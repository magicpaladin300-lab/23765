# RO313 · Dâmbovița County (Județul Dâmbovița), Romania

**UNIT** RO313 (NUTS-3) = Dâmbovița County.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (Law 2/1968): 4,054 km², seat Târgoviște; municipalities Târgoviște and Moreni; towns Fieni, Găești, Pucioasa, Răcari, Titu; 82 communes. The Bucegi and Leaotă mountains in the north (~15%), the Subcarpathian oil hills (~35%) and the Târgoviște and Titu plains (~50%). Historic components: the interwar Dâmbovița County (seat Târgoviște) plus Moreni and its oil district from Prahova County and small exchanges with Argeș and Ilfov. 1950–1968 the territory sat in the Ploiești Region (polygon not used). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: Boian–Gumelnița settlement of the Dâmbovița and Argeș plains.
**Documented-era peak** Window 13, c. 1931: the Moreni and Gura Ocniței oil fields together producing 63% of Romania's crude; band 8 at the lawyered top (range 7–8). Tied at the top by Window 10, c. 1650, Târgoviște as Wallachia's capital on Willcox's floor. Population record 1930–1931 (band 7, held c. 1899–1948).

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 4,054 km² (INS); the mountain north and the highest ridges are excluded: usable plain, terraces and hill valleys 3,000–3,400 km².
2. Cultures at the moment: Boian–Gumelnița on the Dâmbovița, Argeș and Ialomița terraces (Morteni, Titu, Potlogi and the Târgoviște plain findspots), with the Subcarpathian valleys thinly settled.
3. Density 2–3 persons/km² on the usable area, the high end of Müller's (2013) regional Neolithic–Chalcolithic densities by the settlement-area method of Zimmermann, Hilpert & Wendt (2009). Equivalence rule applied. → 6,000–10,200, reported 6–10k.
4. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2 (default agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: farming densities on the mountain and high-hill third; killed any county total above ~14,000 (>4/km² usable).
- Survives: 10,000 people means ~50–100 villages and hamlets of 100–200 along three river corridors at 5–8 km spacing; cropland need under 3% of the usable area.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across Europe's farmed zone c. 4500 BCE gives a few million Europeans against a 5M world floor, a third to a half of humanity. Floor artefact shared with every farmed Old World unit.

## Deep score — Window 13, c. 1931 (documented-era peak)

Numerator components
1. Population on the current territory (INS retrospective series): 354,471 in 1930; 409,272 in 1948; so 355–360k in 1931.
2. GDP multiplier per C12: Romania's ratio to the world in the Depression trough, 0.60–0.65 (Maddison: Romania ~$1,000–1,100 vs world ~$1,650–1,700 per head in 1931–32) × county index 2.0–2.9. Named source: the Romanian petroleum statistics as reported in the US Bureau of Mines reviews of Romanian petroleum development, 1931–1935: in 1931 Moreni held first place with 33.6% of national output and Gura Ocniței second with 29.3%, and in 1933 Gura Ocniței alone furnished 40%; both fields lie inside the polygon. Crude was roughly three fifths of an oil sector worth 7–10% of Romanian GDP, so the county's two fields carried 2.5–4.2% of national product on 1.95% of its population; with the non-oil economy (Târgoviște, the Subcarpathian hill farming) the county reached 3.9–5.6% of GDP, an index of 2.0–2.9. → 0.60–0.65 × 2.0–2.9 = 1.2–1.9.

Denominator: 2,078M at 1931 (rubric points 1918 and 1950, interpolated).

Result: population 0.0171–0.0173% (band 7); GDP 0.0205–0.0329% (band 7–8; 8 at the lawyered top). The 1937 moment (368–376k over 2,212M; Dâmbovița's share of crude down to two fifths to a half as the Prahova fields rose; index 1.6–2.3 × 0.69) gives 0.0183–0.0272%, band 7.

Veto notes
- Killed: a Ruling B valuation at world prices (3.7–4.5 million tons of county crude at ~$1 a barrel in 1931–33, under $35 million of the day), which gives 0.008–0.010% of world GDP for the oil alone and, with the rest of the county, 0.018–0.022%; the route binds below the index route (as for Prahova) and is the consensus column.
- Killed: crediting the Ploiești refining margin on Moreni crude to this county (it is Prahova's).

## Tie — Window 10, c. 1650, Târgoviște as capital (Willcox floor)
Târgoviște was the capital of Wallachia from 1418 to 1659 (co-capital with Bucharest after 1459), seat of the Metropolitan from 1517, Matei Basarab's court and press in 1632–54, with doubled walls, a rebuilt ditch and ten bastions; Paul of Aleppo's diary of 1653–56 describes it at its peak. City 12–25k at the top of the published readings, county 45–75k over Willcox's 470M → 0.0096–0.016% population (band 6–7); GDP with the capital premium (1.6–2.0 × Eastern Europe 0.9–1.0 = 1.44–2.0) → 0.0138–0.0319% (band 6–8). Ties the 1931 top but on a thinner numerator, so 1931 is the record and 1650 the tie. The 1450 and 1500 moments (20–45k over 350M and 425M, same premium) give 0.008–0.023% and 0.008–0.021%, band 6–7.

## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 7.** Nameable command and commodity: Târgoviște as the princely seat of Wallachia for two and a half centuries (1418–1659), and the Moreni–Gura Ocniței field, Romania's largest, which with the Prahova refineries fed the Axis's principal external oil supply in 1940–44. A principality's capital and a shared commodity, so 7 rather than 8.
- **Mind 7.** Nameable institutions: Macarie's press at Târgoviște (1508), the first printing in Wallachia and among the earliest Cyrillic printing anywhere; the Metropolitan see of Wallachia at Târgoviște (1517–1668); Matei Basarab's printed law code (1652) and the Dealu and Chindia monuments. National rank, like Brașov's Coresi and Honterus, so 7.

## Flags
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is the Window 1 floor artefact. The documented-era record is split between the 1931 oil moment (registered-output grade, 0.021–0.033%) and the c. 1650 capital moment (0.014–0.032%); both reach band 8 only at the top of range, and the 1931 figure is preferred as the record for its named statistical source. On the 1937 oil peak the county falls to band 7 because Prahova's fields had overtaken it.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 only (~4–5×); the 1650 figure is ~2–3×.
- **Boundary smearing.** Moreni's transfer from Prahova; all pre-1930 figures are reconstructions.
- **Double-count risk.** RO316 Prahova (the refining of Moreni crude, credited there; its 1937 crude share should be read as two fifths to three fifths given this county's 63% in 1931), RO311 Argeș (the Argeș plain), RO321 Bucharest and RO322 Ilfov (the Titu plain's margins).
- **Citation flags.** No published seventeenth-century population for Târgoviște; the 2022 GDP is inferred from the per-capita ratio.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 7–8 GDP held c. 1925–1940; band 7 population held c. 1899–1948.
