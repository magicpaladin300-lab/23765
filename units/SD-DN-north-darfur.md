# SD-DN · North Darfur State (Wilayat Shamal Darfur), Sudan

**UNIT** SD-DN (ISO 3166-2) = North Darfur State. Scale flag (C14): an ADM1 unit of 296,420 km², forty to sixty times a NUTS-3 county; every share below carries the polygon-size caveat.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (state of 1994, reduced in 2012 only on the Central Darfur side): 296,420 km², seat El Fasher; localities El Fasher, Kutum, Kabkabiya, Mellit, Umm Kaddadah, Al-Lait, Al-Tina, Karnoi, Umm Baru, Saraf Omra, Al-Sireaf, Tawila, Kernoi, Al-Waha, Kelemando, Tawisha, Dar al-Salam. The northern Darfur plateau from Jebel Marra's northern slopes and the Tagabo and Meidob hills to the Libyan Desert, the Wadi Howar's middle course and the Zaghawa and Berti country. Historic components: the Tunjur capitals of Uri and Ain Farah, the Keira sultanate's seats at Shoba and El Fasher and its caravan town of Kobbei, the Anglo-Egyptian Darfur Province (El Fasher), Darfur Province (1956–94). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the Wadi Howar ("Yellow Nile") and the plateau's hills in the African Humid Period at the equivalence-rule densities.
**Documented-era peak** Window 11, c. 1790: the Darfur sultanate's capital region, with Kobbei at the southern end of the Darb al-Arba'in (the Keira's first commercial and political capital), El Fasher founded as the royal seat in 1791–92 and Browne's visit of 1793–96, when Darfur was Egypt's largest trading partner south of the Sahara; band 10 at the lawyered top (range 8–10), tied by c. 1750 (Sultan Tayrab's seat at Shoba) on Willcox's floor. Census-grade peak: band 8 (population 2008–2024).

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 296,420 km²: the Wadi Howar corridor and its tributaries and the Tagabo, Meidob and northern Jebel Marra hills (~20,000 km²), the best-watered ground of the eastern Sahara in the African Humid Period (c. 9000–3500 BCE), when the Wadi Howar flowed seasonally to the Nile; the plateau and desert (~276,000 km²), savanna to semi-desert then.
2. Communities at the moment: the Wadi Howar's herder-fisher-gatherers (the Khartoum-Variant-related and early Leiterband groups; the Leiterband sites of c. 4000–2200 BCE are the densest Neolithic record of the eastern Sahara) and the hills' pastoralists; the equivalence rule for a non-literate society applies at the high end.
3. Density, equivalence rule, high end: 0.3–0.8 persons/km² on the wadi corridor and hills, 0.03–0.1 on the plateau and desert (Hassan's Saharan Neolithic range, C14). → 6–16k on the corridor, 8–28k elsewhere, 14–44k in all.
4. GDP multiplier 0.8–1.0 (pastoral).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: densities above 0.1/km² off the wadi and hills; killed a total above ~50,000.
- Survives: 44,000 people means ~220–440 camps and hamlets of 100 along the Wadi Howar, its tributaries and the hills; grazing need under 5% of the polygon.
- Allocation flag: two thirds of the numerator is a plateau allocation; the Wadi Howar record anchors the rest.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across the humid-period Sahara gives 1–2 million people against a 5M world floor, a fifth to two fifths of humanity; with the Nile and Europe's farmed zone at the same stacking the regions exceed the floor. The floor artefact is at its most extreme for the largest polygons.

## Deep score — Window 11, c. 1790 (documented-era peak; c. 1750 ties)

Numerator components
1. Built numerators: Kobbei, the terminus of the Darb al-Arba'in and the sultanate's commercial capital, which Browne found the largest town of Darfur (its merchants' quarters of several thousand houses), 10–20k; El Fasher, the new royal seat (1791–92), 5–10k with the court; Kabkabiya, Kutum, Mellit and the market villages 5–10k. Towns 20–40k.
2. Countryside: the northern Fur, Zaghawa, Berti, Tunjur and Meidob dars, the sultanate's most populous provinces after Jebel Marra, 30–40% of a sultanate lawyered at 1.0–1.5M (the 1955–56 census found 1.33M in Darfur after the Mahdist losses; Browne's 200,000 of the 1790s is the low bound in credentialed print) → 280–560k. County 300–600k.
3. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.4: Maddison's Africa ratio (~0.7 of world c. 1790) × role premium 1.4–2.0 relative to the region for the royal capital and the Darb al-Arba'in terminus, whose caravans of up to 2,000 camels carried slaves, ivory, ostrich feathers, gum and tamarind to Asyut and made Darfur Egypt's largest trading partner south of the Sahara ("capital/entrepôt argued"; Browne describes the trade but gives no customs total, so the 2.0–2.5 band is not claimed).

Denominator: 858.8M at 1790 (Willcox interpolation 1750–1850; C2 deep floor); 694M at 1750 (Willcox), when Sultan Tayrab's court sat at Shoba north of Jebel Marra.

Result: population 0.035–0.070% (band 8–9); GDP 0.035–0.098% (band 8–10; 0.063% at the mid-range of 450k × 1.2, band 9; 10 at the lawyered top). c. 1750 on Willcox's 694M: 250–550k × 1.0–1.4 → 0.036–0.079% population, 0.036–0.111% GDP (band 8–10).

Veto notes
- Killed: a polygon share above 40% of the sultanate (Jebel Marra's west, the south and Dar Masalit take the rest; the Darfur units' tops over-allocate together, flagged under C11); killed a multiplier above 1.4 (the caravan trade's value was a few tenths of the agrarian product).
- Killed: counting Egypt's side of the trade or the Kordofan tribute after 1785.
- Allocation flag: the countryside is an allocation of an estimated sultanate total; the towns are built from Browne's description.

## Flags
- **Scale (C14).** An ADM1 state of 296,420 km²; at NUTS-3 scale (El Fasher locality) its Window 1 share would be a fiftieth and its sultanate share a third. Every band above is a state-level band.
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is a Window 1 allocation (two thirds plateau). The documented-era record is band 10 only at the top of range (600k people and the 1.4× multiplier together), band 9 at the mid-range; the 1750 and 1790 moments tie, and the Darfur units' lawyered tops together over-allocate the sultanate (Central 25–35%, North 30–40%, South 15–25%, West and East the rest). The census-grade series is band 8 since 2008.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 (~7–8×); the c. 1790 GDP figure (~4×). The contemporary figures are 1.0–1.1×.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-1994 figures are allocations from Darfur Province and the sultanate; the 2012 transfer to Central Darfur was small on this side.
- **Double-count risk.** SD-DC Central Darfur (Jebel Marra's west and the sultanate's early seat), SD-DW West Darfur (Kabkabiya's west, Dar Masalit), SD-DE and SD-DS (the sultanate's south), Egypt's Asyut and New Valley (the Darb al-Arba'in's northern half), Chad's Wadi Fira and Ennedi (the Zaghawa dar).
- **Citation flags.** No sultanate population total was retrieved (Browne's 200,000 is the low bound); Kobbei's size is from Browne's description, not a count; the 2024 population and the GDP shares are estimates.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 in Window 1 and, at the top of range only, c. 1750–1800; band 8–9 held c. 1700–1874 on the lawyered sultanate figures; band 8 on population since 2008.
