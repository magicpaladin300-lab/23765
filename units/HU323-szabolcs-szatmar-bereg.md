# HU323 · Szabolcs-Szatmár-Bereg county, Hungary

**UNIT** HU323 (NUTS-3) = Szabolcs-Szatmár-Bereg county. **Polygon** current boundaries (1950 merger): 5,936 km², seat Nyíregyháza. Formed from Szabolcs county (4,637 km², 319,818 people in 1910) less the Polgár strip given to Hajdú-Bihar, plus the parts of Szatmár, Bereg and Ugocsa counties left in Hungary after 1920. Divergences of a year or less ignored.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: Tiszapolgár horizon on the Upper Tisza levees, the Rétköz and Bereg plains and the Nyírség margins. **Documented-era peak** Window 12, c. 1900–1910.
**WINNING PATHWAY** GDP (population × 1.0–1.2); population practically tied.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE
Numerator: land split of 5,936 km²: Tisza, Szamos and Kraszna levees, Rétköz and Bereg margins, Nyírség edge loess (~2,000 km²) at 1.5–2.5 persons/km²; Nyírség sand interior (~3,000 km²) at 0.5–1; Ecsed marsh and Rétköz interior (~900 km²) at 0.2–0.5. Pegs as HU332 (KRAP hamlet pattern; Raczky's Öcsöd densities; Müller 2013; Zimmermann–Hilpert–Wendt 2009), discounted for the sand and the marsh; equivalence-rule high end within each zone. The Upper Tisza is a core area of the Tiszapolgár and Bodrogkeresztúr cultures, with the type site just across the county line at Polgár. → 4,700–8,450 people. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2.
Denominator: 5.0M (rubric floors table). No lower published figure.
Vetoes: killed, Körös-region densities on the Nyírség sand and the Ecsed marsh (drained only in 1898); killed, anything above ~9,000. Survives: 1.5–2.5/km² on the levees and loess, consistent with the Upper Tisza Neolithic and Copper Age site record (Méhtelek, Tiszabezdéd, Tiszadob, Nagykálló).

## Deep score — c. 1900–1910 (documented-era peak)
Numerator: Szabolcs 319,818 (1910; ≈290,000 in 1900) less the Polgár strip (≈25,000), plus the Hungarian parts of Szatmár (≈115,000 in 1910, scaled from the 1920 rump county), Bereg (≈27,000) and Ugocsa (≈2,000) → present territory ≈395–405k (1900), ≈434–444k (1910). Cross-check: present-territory 1980 = 593,829. Denominator 1,564M (Maddison 2003). Population 0.025% (band 7). GDP: Maddison Hungary 1900 at 1.33× world; county at 0.85–1.0× Hungary (Nyírség tobacco, potato and later apple; Nyíregyháza market town; the poorest large county of the Kingdom's core) → 1.13–1.33× → 0.029–0.034% (band 8, lower bound on the 8/7 line).

## Candidate — Window 2/3 boundary, c. 1200 BCE
Gáva culture (eponymous Gáva-Katóhalom) and the Upper Tisza hoard horizon (Opályi, Nyírtura, Kemecse, Nagykálló), the densest Late Bronze Age bronze-hoard concentration in Europe; 2–4/km² on the prime zone, 1–2 elsewhere → 9–18k over 45.4M = 0.02–0.04% population, 0.016–0.048% GDP → band 7–8. Not fully lawyered.

## Qualitative
- **Strategy 6.** Záhony–Csap rail transshipment complex, the main broad-gauge to standard-gauge gateway between the Soviet Union and Central Europe (1950s–1980s) and again the EU–Ukraine rail gateway since 2022; the Szabolcs earthwork of the 10th century. Below the Suez peg; no sole command.
- **Mind 5.** Máriapócs weeping icon (1696), the national Greek Catholic shrine; Nagykálló as a Hasidic pilgrimage site; the Synod of Szabolcs (1092) is event fame; the Upper Tisza hoards as a Bronze Age metalwork landscape.

## Anchor comparisons
All-time peak (0.09–0.20%): overlaps HU312 Heves (0.11–0.19%) and Potosí c. 1600 (R4, 0.15–0.2% GDP), below HU332, HU321 and HU322. Documented-era peak (0.029–0.034%): just below HU332 and HU321 at 1900, above Cahokia (R6).

## Flags
- Floor fragility as for HU332: on a 7M denominator the peak is 0.07–0.12%, band 9–10.
- Thin lawyering (delta >3×) for windows 1–3.
- Boundary smearing: the Szatmár, Bereg and Ugocsa rump reconstructions for 1900/1910 are mine; the 1949 present-territory figure was not retrieved.
- The 2022 county GDP is an estimate (the figure in the web listing, 3,996 bn HUF, is inconsistent with the county's per-capita rank and was not used).
- Double-count risk: the Polgár strip is counted under HU321 and excluded here; the Upper Tisza prehistoric landscape continues into Borsod-Abaúj-Zemplén, not on the list.
- Ruling D: band 10 only in window 1; band 7–8 held c. 1870–1950.
