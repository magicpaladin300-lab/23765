# HU322 · Jász-Nagykun-Szolnok county, Hungary

**UNIT** HU322 (NUTS-3) = Jász-Nagykun-Szolnok county. **Polygon** current boundaries (1950 reform): 5,582 km², seat Szolnok. The historic county (5,251 km², 373,964 people in 1910) gained the Tiszafüred district from Heves and the Abony–Újszász–Tószeg group from Pest, and lost Dévaványa to Békés. Divergences of a year or less ignored.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the Tiszazug and Jászság at the Late Tisza to Tiszapolgár transition (Öcsöd-Kováshalom, Szolnok-Szanda). **Documented-era peak** Window 12, c. 1900.
**WINNING PATHWAY** GDP (population × 1.0–1.2); population practically tied.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE
Numerator: land split of 5,582 km²: Jászság loess, Tiszazug, Tisza levees and the Tiszafüred basin (~4,200 km²) at 2–3 persons/km²; Nagykunság alkali steppe and Sárrét margins (~1,400 km²) at 1–1.5. Pegs as HU332, and the density peg itself lies inside this polygon: Öcsöd-Kováshalom, 136–204 persons/ha in the fenced core and 230–250 people on the 3 ha occupied at once (Raczky). Equivalence-rule high end. → 9,800–14,700 people. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2.
Denominator: 5.0M (rubric floors table). No lower published figure.
Vetoes: killed, extending Öcsöd's core density over its whole 20+ ha scatter; killed, plain densities on the Nagykunság alkali flats; killed, anything above ~16,500 (>3/km²). Survives: 2–3/km² on the loess and levees, consistent with the Szolnok county archaeological topography and the Tiszazug surveys.

## Deep score — c. 1900 (documented-era peak)
Numerator: historic county 373,964 (1910; ≈345,000 in 1900) minus Dévaványa (≈13,600) plus the Tiszafüred district (≈30,000) and the Pest transfers (Abony, Újszász, Tószeg, Zagyvarékas, Szászberek, Jászkarajenő, Kőröstetétlen, ≈35,000) → present territory ≈390–400k (1900), ≈420–430k (1910). Cross-check: present-territory 1980 = 445,624. Denominator 1,564M (Maddison 2003). Population 0.025% (band 7). GDP: Maddison Hungary 1900 at 1.33× world; county at 1.0–1.1× Hungary (Szolnok as the Tisza rail and river hub since the Pest–Szolnok line of 1847; Jászság and Nagykunság grain) → 1.33–1.46× → 0.033–0.037% (band 8).

## Candidate — Window 2, c. 1600 BCE
Tószeg-Laposhalom, the reference tell of Central European Bronze Age chronology; Tiszafüred-Ásotthalom tell and its 1,200-grave cemetery; Jászdózsa-Kápolnahalom; 2–4/km² → 11–22k over 36.2M = 0.03–0.06% population, 0.024–0.073% GDP → band 8–9. Not fully lawyered.

## Qualitative
- **Strategy 5.** Szolnok fortress and Ottoman sanjak (1552–1685); the Tisza crossing and rail junction; Priscus's "between the Tisza and the Körös" for Attila's camp fits the Tiszazug better than any neighbour, but remains unlocated. No ≥8.
- **Mind 5.** The Jász (Alanic) and Cuman settlements of the 13th century, the only Iranian-speaking community of medieval Central Europe (Jászberény, the Lehel horn); Tószeg as a type site; the Tiszafüred Avar cemetery (1,282 graves).

## Anchor comparisons
All-time peak (0.20–0.35%): overlaps HU332 (0.22–0.41%) and HU321 (0.19–0.35%), above Potosí (R4), below Angkor (R5). Documented-era peak (0.033–0.037%): level with HU332 and HU321 at 1900, above Cahokia (R6).

## Flags
- Floor fragility as for HU332: on a 7M denominator the peak is 0.14–0.21%, still band 10.
- Thin lawyering (delta >3×) for windows 1–2.
- Boundary smearing: the 1950 transfers for 1900/1910 are my reconstruction; the 1949 present-territory figure was not retrieved.
- Double-count risk: the Tiszafüred district is counted here and excluded from HU312; Dévaványa is counted under HU332 and excluded here; the Tiszazug prehistoric landscape continues into HU333 and Bács-Kiskun.
- Ruling D: band 10 only in window 1; band 7–8 held c. 1850–1950.
