# RO415 · Vâlcea County (Județul Vâlcea), Romania

**UNIT** RO415 (NUTS-3) = Vâlcea County.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (Law 2/1968): 5,765 km², seat Râmnicu Vâlcea; municipalities Râmnicu Vâlcea and Drăgășani; towns Băbeni, Băile Govora, Băile Olănești, Bălcești, Berbești, Brezoi, Călimănești, Horezu, Ocnele Mari; 78 communes. The Olt gorge through the Carpathians, the Căpățânii, Lotru and Cozia mountains in the north (~35%), the Subcarpathian hills with the Ocnele Mari salt dome (~40%) and the Getic piedmont down to the Drăgășani vineyards (~25%). Historic components: the interwar Vâlcea County (seat Râmnicu Vâlcea) plus the Bălcești area from Romanați and small exchanges with Argeș and Gorj. 1950–1968 the territory sat in the Argeș Region (polygon not used). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: Sălcuța–Gumelnița settlement of the Olt terraces and the Subcarpathian valleys, with the Ocnele Mari brine springs already worked.
**Documented-era peak** Band 7 at the lawyered top (range 6–7) on three near-level moments: c. 1650–1700 (the Ocnele Mari salt monopoly on Willcox's floor), 1850 (Willcox floor) and 1912–1930 (population). No single moment dominates.
**WINNING PATHWAY** GDP in Window 1 (population tied); GDP c. 1650–1700, population 1930 in the documented era.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 5,765 km² (INS); the mountain third is excluded: usable hills, terraces and piedmont 3,000–3,500 km².
2. Cultures at the moment: Sălcuța (the Oltenian Chalcolithic) meeting Gumelnița on the lower Olt; Copăcelu-Valea Răii and the Govora valley among the published settlements; the brine springs of Ocnele Mari exploited from the Neolithic (Chalcolithic briquetage at Ocnele Mari-Copăcelu).
3. Density 2–3 persons/km² on the usable area, the high end of Müller's (2013) regional Neolithic–Chalcolithic densities by the settlement-area method of Zimmermann, Hilpert & Wendt (2009). Equivalence rule applied. → 6,000–10,500, reported 6–10k.
4. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2 (default agrarian; salt production is attested but unquantified, so no extraction premium).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: farming densities on the mountain third; killed any county total above ~14,000 (>4/km² usable).
- Survives: 10,000 people means ~50–100 villages and hamlets of 100–200 along the Olt, Olteț, Cerna and Govora valleys at 5–8 km spacing; cropland need under 3% of the usable area.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across Europe's farmed zone c. 4500 BCE gives a few million Europeans against a 5M world floor, a third to a half of humanity. Floor artefact shared with every farmed Old World unit.

## Deep score — Window 10, c. 1650–1700, the salt monopoly (documented-era GDP peak)

Numerator components
1. County population 35–55k c. 1650 and 40–60k c. 1700 (Wallachia at 0.5–0.8M; the Vâlcea district among its more populous hill districts; Râmnic a bishopric from 1503–04 and a market town).
2. GDP multiplier. Ocnele Mari was the principal salt mine of Wallachia from the fifteenth century (the 1422 grant of Radu Praznaglava; Wavrin's 1445 notice), one of only three extra-Carpathian mines working in the seventeenth century (with Slănic and Teișani), and salt was a princely monopoly whose revenue the treasury registers of Constantin Brâncoveanu (the *Condica Vistieriei*, 1694–1703) record as one of the largest items of state income, exported in bulk down the Olt and the Danube to the Ottoman Balkans. Named-source premium 1.6–2.0 (the rubric's entrepôt band, argued for the principality's chief export commodity) × Eastern Europe's per-capita ratio to the world 0.9–1.0 → 1.44–2.0. The 2.0–2.5 "quantified fiscal documentation" band was considered and not applied county-wide, because the registers' salt totals could not be read in this session.

Denominators: 470M at 1650 (Willcox 1931, C2 deep floor, citation flag open); 610M at 1700 (McEvedy & Jones).

Result: c. 1650, population 0.0074–0.0117% (band 6), GDP 0.0107–0.0234% (band 6–7); c. 1700, population 0.0066–0.0098%, GDP 0.0094–0.0197% (band 6–7).

Veto notes
- Killed: a Ruling B valuation of the salt at world prices; tens of thousands of tons a year at a few dollars a ton (1990 prices) is under 0.001% of world GDP.
- Killed: counting the Telega and Slănic salt (Prahova) in Vâlcea's monopoly figure.

## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 6.** The Olt gorge as the Transylvania–Wallachia road (Turnu Roșu pass, the Roman Limes Alutanus and the 1916 fighting, events aside); Ocnele Mari as Wallachia's principal salt mine and export commodity for five centuries, a nameable commodity of national rank; the Lotru hydropower scheme (1972) and the Râmnicu Vâlcea chemical works (Oltchim) under communism. No chokepoint of more than regional reach, so 6.
- **Mind 7.** Nameable institutions: the Govora press (1637–42) and its *Pravila de la Govora* (1640), the first printed law code in Romanian; the Râmnic press under Antim Ivireanul (1705–08) and the eighteenth-century "capital of Romanian typography" that printed for the Orthodox Serbs and Bulgarians; Cozia (1388) and Horezu (1690, the type monument of the Brâncovenesc style, UNESCO). National rank with regional reach beyond Romania, so 7.

## Flags
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is the Window 1 floor artefact. The documented-era record is spread over three near-level moments (c. 1650 salt, 1850, 1930) that all reach band 7 only at the top of range; the salt premium is argued, not read from the registers.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 only (~4–5×); the c. 1650 figure is ~2–3×.
- **Boundary smearing.** All pre-1948 figures are reconstructions; the 1930 total on the current territory is ±10k.
- **Double-count risk.** RO414 Olt (the Limes Alutanus and Romula's province, scored there), RO316 Prahova (the Slănic and Telega salt), RO126 Sibiu (the Turnu Roșu pass), RO412 Gorj and RO411 Dolj (the Sălcuța landscape).
- **Citation flags.** The *Condica Vistieriei* salt totals were not read; the 2022 GDP is inferred from the county's share.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 6–7 held c. 1650–1948.
