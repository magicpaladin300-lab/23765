# RO317 · Teleorman County (Județul Teleorman), Romania

**UNIT** RO317 (NUTS-3) = Teleorman County.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (Law 2/1968): 5,790 km², seat Alexandria; municipalities Alexandria, Roșiorii de Vede, Turnu Măgurele; towns Videle, Zimnicea; 92 communes. Entirely lowland: the Boian and Găvanu-Burdea plains between the Olt and the Vlașca forest-steppe, with the Danube frontage at Turnu Măgurele and Zimnicea. Historic components: the interwar Teleorman County (347,427 in 1930; seat Turnu Măgurele 1839–1950 and 1952–1968) plus the western plăși of Vlașca County (Videle, Drăgănești-Vlașca and neighbours, ≈70k in 1930). 1950–1968 the territory sat in the Bucharest Region (polygon not used). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the Boian–Gumelnița transition on the Vedea, Teleorman, Călmățui and Olt valleys.
**Documented-era peak** Window 12, 1912 (GDP); population record 1930 (Window 13). Band 7 held c. 1900–1956.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 5,790 km² (INS), all plain; usable area taken as the whole polygon.
2. Cultures at the moment: the Boian (Spanțov) to Gumelnița A1 transition. The Vedea–Teleorman corridor alone holds almost forty Gumelnița tells (Andreescu & Mirea; the Southern Romania Archaeological Project of Bailey, Andreescu, Mills and Trick on the Teleorman valley, 1998–2004: Măgura-Buduiasca, Lăceni, Vitănești-Măgurice). The tells are small (20–100 m across, 1–10 m high; Vitănești 40 × 45 m, 6.5 m) and sit in the floodplains against the terraces.
3. Density 2–3 persons/km², the high end of Müller's (2013) regional Neolithic–Chalcolithic densities by the settlement-area method of Zimmermann, Hilpert & Wendt (2009), applied to the whole polygon. Equivalence rule applied. → 11,600–17,400, reported 12–18k.
4. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2 (default agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: counting the forty-odd Vedea–Teleorman tells as simultaneous villages of 200–300. The tells are 0.05–0.8 ha and sequentially occupied across Boian, Gumelnița A1, A2 and B1, so each held 30–150 people at a time.
- Killed: any county total above ~23,000 (>4/km²), which no regional synthesis supports.
- Survives: 18,000 people means ~90–180 hamlets and tells of 100–200 at 5–8 km spacing on four river corridors, consistent with the recorded tell density; cropland need under 3% of the polygon.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across Europe's farmed zone c. 4500 BCE gives a few million Europeans against a 5M world floor, a third to a half of humanity. Floor artefact shared with every farmed Old World unit.

## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 5.** The Danube frontier in three editions: Roman Turris and the Limes Transalutanus (c. 106–245), the Ottoman kaza of Turnu (1417–1829, one of the three Wallachian Danube rayas), and the Zimnicea–Svishtov crossing used by the Russian army in June 1877 (the crossing point is structural, the campaign is an event). No chokepoint of more than regional reach.
- **Mind 4.** The Boian–Gumelnița tell landscape of the Teleorman valley as the type region of the Lower Danube Chalcolithic (SRAP); the Getic necropolis of Zimnicea. Marin Preda's Siliștea-Gumești and the setting of *Moromeții* fall under the event-fame ban.

## Flags
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is the Window 1 floor artefact. The documented-era record is split: GDP peaks in 1912 on Maddison's high Romanian 1913 figure, population in 1930; both band 7, and band 7 holds from c. 1900 to 1956 on the population pathway.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 only (~4–5×).
- **Boundary smearing.** Figures before 1930 are back-projections from the 1930 current-territory total; the Vlașca plăși are ±10k.
- **Double-count risk.** RO314 Giurgiu (the rest of Vlașca), RO414 Olt (the Olt valley and the 1877 Corabia crossing), RO312 Călărași (the Boian type site). The Getic allocation must sum to ≤100% of Dacia across Romanian units.
- **Citation flags.** The 1912 and 1899 county totals on the current territory are reconstructed, not read from the INS series; the 2022 GDP is inferred from the county's ~1% national share.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 7 population held c. 1900–1956; band 7 GDP only c. 1900–1912.
