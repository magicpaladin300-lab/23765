# SD-NW · White Nile State (Wilayat an-Nil al-Abyad), Sudan

**UNIT** SD-NW (ISO 3166-2) = White Nile State. Scale flag (C14): an ADM1 unit of 39,701 km², five to eight times a NUTS-3 county; every share below carries the polygon-size caveat.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (state of 1994): 39,701 km², seat Rabak; localities Rabak, Kosti, Ed Dueim, Al-Jabalain, Kenana, Umm Rimta, Tendelti, Al-Salam, Guli (Al-Qutaina). The White Nile from the South Sudan border to below Ed Dueim, with the western Gezira clay plain and the Kenana, Assalaya and White Nile sugar estates. Historic components: Alodia's and the Funj sultanate's western Gezira (Alays), the Shilluk kingdom's northern marches, Aba Island (the Mahdi's base, 1881), the Turco-Egyptian and condominium White Nile Province (Ed Dueim), Blue Nile and White Nile provinces (1956–94). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the Khartoum Neolithic fisher-herders of the White Nile banks and the humid-period plain at the equivalence-rule densities.
**Documented-era peak** Window 16, 2018–2024: the Central Bureau of Statistics projection of 2,493,880 (2018) carried to 2.9–3.2M in 2024 with the displaced of the 2023– war, on the Kenana–Kosti sugar and river-port economy; band 8 (census-grade on the CBS projection). Allocation-grade candidate: Alodia's western Gezira c. 550–900 (band 7–9), not adopted.
**WINNING PATHWAY** Population (GDP tied in Window 1; GDP band 5–6 today).

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 39,701 km²: the White Nile's banks and terraces (~4,000 km²) and the clay plain of the western Gezira (~35,700 km²), savanna in the African Humid Period.
2. Communities at the moment: the Khartoum Neolithic (Shaheinab horizon) along the White Nile, with the Shabona and Rabak sites in the polygon, herder-fisher-cultivators; the equivalence rule for a non-literate society applies at the high end.
3. Density, equivalence rule, high end: 1–2 persons/km² on the river strip (the Shaheinab-type sites are large and close-set), 0.15–0.4 on the plain (Hassan's Saharan–Sahelian Neolithic range, C14). → 4–8k on the river, 5.4–14.3k on the plain, 9.4–22.3k in all.
4. GDP multiplier 0.8–1.1 (riverine herder-cultivators with cattle wealth).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: Nile-floodplain densities above 2/km² for a fisher-herder horizon; killed a total above ~28,000.
- Survives: 22,300 people means ~110–220 villages, camps and hamlets of 100, a third of them on the river at 3–5 km spacing; the Shaheinab-type site lists support the riverine figure.
- Allocation flag: the plain is an allocation.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking along the Nile and across the humid-period Sahel gives 2–3 million people against a 5M world floor, roughly half of humanity.

## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 6.** Nameable but national: Kenana (1981), one of the world's largest integrated sugar estates and Sudan's largest agro-industrial complex, with Assalaya and White Nile Sugar; Kosti, the White Nile's river port and the Khartoum–El Obeid railway's river crossing (1910); Aba Island, where the Mahdiyya began (1881) and the Ansar were crushed in 1970; the Jebelain–Renk corridor to South Sudan. No chokepoint beyond the region.
- **Mind 6.** Nameable claim: Aba Island, the Mahdi's retreat and the birthplace of the Mahdist movement (1881), and the Ansar's seat under the Mahdi's family; the Shabona and Rabak Neolithic sites of the Khartoum tradition; the Dueim schools. One national-scale religious landmark, so 6.

## Flags
- **Scale (C14).** An ADM1 state of 39,701 km²; at NUTS-3 scale its shares would be a fifth to an eighth. Every band above is a state-level band.
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is a Window 1 allocation anchored on the Khartoum Neolithic. The documented-era record is census-grade only through the CBS projection (the last census is 2008); the Alodian candidate would reach band 9 at the top but has no built numerator inside the polygon.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 (~6–7×); the Alodian figure (~3×). The contemporary figures are 1.0–1.2×.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-1994 figures are allocations from Blue Nile and White Nile provinces and from the Funj; the South Sudanese refugees in the Al-Jabalain camps are inside the 2024 upper bound.
- **Double-count risk.** SD-KH Khartoum (Soba, Jebel Aulia, the displaced), SD-GZ Gezira (the Funj core and the irrigated Gezira), SD-SI Sennar (the Funj capital), SD-KN North Kordofan (Kosti's western hinterland), South Sudan's Upper Nile (Renk, the Shilluk).
- **Citation flags.** The 2024 population and the GDP shares are estimates; no Alodian or Funj population figure for the western Gezira was retrieved.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 8 on population from c. 2016 on the CBS trend; band 7 from the 1970s.
