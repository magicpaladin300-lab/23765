# SD-KN · North Kordofan State (Wilayat Shamal Kurdufan), Sudan

**UNIT** SD-KN (ISO 3166-2) = North Kordofan State. Scale flag (C14): an ADM1 unit of 185,302 km² (the figure carried for the state; the 2013 re-establishment of West Kordofan took back An-Nuhud and its localities, so the current polygon may be somewhat smaller, flagged), thirty to fifty times a NUTS-3 county; every share below carries the polygon-size caveat.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (state of 1994, as adjusted in 2005 and 2013): seat El Obeid (Al-Ubayyid); localities Sheikan (El Obeid), Bara, Umm Ruwaba, Ar-Rahad, Sodiri, Jebrat ash-Sheikh, Umm Dam Haj Ahmed, Gharb Bara. The qoz sands of central Kordofan (El Obeid, Bara, Umm Ruwaba, the gum arabic belt), the Kababish steppe and desert of the north (Sodiri, Hamrat ash-Sheikh, Wadi al-Milk, Jebel Haraza). Historic components: the Ghudiyat and Bedeiria chiefdoms under Funj suzerainty, the Musabba'at sultanate of Kordofan (c. 1740s–1785, Bara and the El Obeid district), Darfur's Kordofan (1785–1821), Turco-Egyptian Kordofan with El Obeid as capital (1821–83), the Mahdiyya (El Obeid taken 1883), condominium Kordofan Province (El Obeid, Bara, Dar Kababish, Dar Hamid districts), Kordofan Province (1956–74), North Kordofan (1974–). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the humid-period savanna and steppe of central and northern Kordofan, with the Wadi al-Milk and Jebel Haraza, at the equivalence-rule densities over the gallery's second-largest polygon.
**Documented-era peak** Window 11, c. 1750: the Musabba'at sultanate of Kordofan (the Keira cadet line expelled from Darfur, ruling from Bara and the El Obeid district under Isawi and then Hashim), the gum arabic and slave trade on the Darfur–Sennar route, documented in the Funj and Darfur chronicles and in O'Fahey and Spaulding's Kingdoms of the Sudan; band 9 at the lawyered top (range 7–9, Willcox's 694M floor). Census-grade peak: band 8 (population 1983–2024; CBS 2018 estimate 3,174,029 → 0.0417%; 2024 trend 0.044–0.048%). Contemporary GDP: band 6 (PPP, estimated).

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 185,302 km²: the northern steppe and desert (~90,000 km², north of about 15°N: the Kababish country, Wadi al-Milk, Jebel Haraza), savanna-steppe with seasonal lakes in the African Humid Period; the central qoz (~95,300 km²: El Obeid, Bara, Umm Ruwaba, Ar-Rahad), savanna.
2. Communities at the moment: Saharan–Sahelian pastoral Neolithic herders along the Wadi al-Milk (a humid-period tributary of the Nile, with Neolithic sites on its course to Ed Debba) and around Jebel Haraza (rock art), herder-cultivators on the qoz; no excavated settlement inside the polygon is known to me, so the equivalence rule for a non-literate society applies at the high end of the regional density range (C14), with the Wadi Howar and Khartoum Neolithic sequences as regional anchors.
3. Density, equivalence rule, high end: 0.1–0.25 persons/km² on the northern steppe, 0.15–0.4 on the central savanna (Hassan's Saharan–Sahelian Neolithic range, C14). → 9–22.5k north, 14.3–38.1k centre, 23.3–60.6k in all.
4. GDP multiplier 0.8–1.0 (herder economy with cattle wealth, no towns).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: savanna densities above 0.4/km² and steppe densities above 0.25/km²; killed a total above ~65,000.
- Survives: 60,600 people means ~500–600 camps and hamlets of 100 over 185,000 km², one per ~300 km², a mobile-herder landscape with clusters on the Wadi al-Milk and around the hills.
- Allocation flag: the whole figure is an allocation; rock art and surface finds anchor occupation, not numbers.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking along the Nile and across the humid-period Sahel gives 2–3 million people against a 5M world floor, roughly half of humanity; the Kordofan units (SD-GK, SD-KS, SD-KN) together claim ~1.5–3.4% of the world in this window, a polygon-size effect flagged in each.

## Deep score — Window 11, c. 1750 (documented-era peak)

Numerator components
1. The Musabba'at sultanate of Kordofan: the Keira cadet line that lost Darfur's throne settled in Kordofan in the 1740s and built a state over the Ghudiyat, Bedeiria, Dar Hamid and the Kababish, with its seat at Bara and in the El Obeid district (the later Turco-Egyptian capital was founded on its market), tributary to and at war with Sennar and Darfur in turn until Darfur's conquest in 1785. Kordofan's whole population before 1821 lawyered at 400–700k (the Turco-Egyptian poll-tax rolls, Pallme's 1838 account of a populous province, Reclus's post-famine 300,000 as the floor of the floor); the north-central half to three fifths → 200–420k. Bara and the El Obeid market 10–20k.
2. GDP multiplier 0.8–1.3: Maddison's Africa ratio (~0.7 of world c. 1750) × role premium 1.2–1.9 relative to the region for the seat of a sultanate on the Darfur–Sennar route, the Kordofan gum arabic that reached Cairo through Darfur and Egypt, the slave and ivory transit and the Kababish camel trade. Capital premium argued (rubric 1.6–2.0 band, stacked once per C12); no ledger, so the 2.0–2.5 band is not claimed.

Denominator: 694M at 1750 (Willcox 1931; C2 deep floor); McEvedy & Jones's 720M would lower the shares by 4%.

Result: population 0.0288–0.0605% (band 8–9); GDP 0.0231–0.0787% (band 7–9; 0.0469% at the mid-range of 310k × 1.05, band 8; 9 at the lawyered top). c. 1790 on 858.8M (Darfur's Kordofan; El Obeid's market under Darfur's governors; Browne's decade): 200–450k × 0.7–1.2 → 0.0233–0.0524% population, 0.0163–0.0629% GDP (band 7–9). c. 1880 on the 1881 floor 1,375M (Turco-Egyptian El Obeid, the largest town of the Sudan west of the Nile, 50–100k before the Mahdist siege of 1882–83, 35,000 by 1900; the hinterland 300–450k): 350–550k × 0.9–1.4 → 0.0255–0.040% population (band 7–8), 0.0229–0.056% GDP (band 7–9).

## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 7.** Nameable command: the Battle of Shaykan (El Obeid, 3–5 November 1883), where the Mahdi's army annihilated Hicks Pasha's 10,000 and turned the Mahdiyya into a state, one of the 19th century's decisive colonial defeats; the siege and fall of El Obeid (1882–83); the Musabba'at sultanate and Darfur's Kordofan; El Obeid as the Turco-Egyptian and condominium capital of Kordofan, the railhead (1912) and the hub of western Sudan's roads, the world's gum arabic market; the 2023–25 siege of El Obeid. Not 8: a provincial, not a national, command.
- **Mind 5.** Nameable claims: El Obeid's gum arabic market as the price-setter for a world commodity; the Catholic cathedral of El Obeid (1960s) and the Comboni missions; the University of Kordofan (1990); Talal Asad's The Kababish Arabs (1970), a classic of anthropology; the Jebel Haraza rock art. Regional reach, so 5.

## Flags
- **Scale (C14).** An ADM1 state of ~185,000 km²; at NUTS-3 scale its shares would be a thirtieth to a fiftieth. Every band above is a state-level band; the all-time rank (the gallery's largest Window 1 share) is a polygon-size effect, flagged as such.
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is a pure Window 1 allocation with no site anchor. The documented-era record (c. 1750) reaches band 9 only at the top of range (420k people and the 1.3× multiplier on Willcox's floor), band 8 at the mid-range; c. 1650 would be a hair higher but has no documented polity. The census-grade record is band 8 throughout 1983–2024 and does not depend on the lawyering.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 (~6–8×); the c. 1750 GDP figure (~3×). The census-grade figures are 1.0–1.2×.
- **Boundary smearing.** The state's area figure (185,302 km²) predates the 2013 re-split; all pre-1994 figures are allocations from Kordofan Province and its districts; the 2008 count is of the larger 2006–13 state and is allocated to the current polygon.
- **Double-count risk.** SD-GK West Kordofan (An-Nuhud and Dar Hamar, counted in the 2008 North Kordofan total), SD-KS South Kordofan (the northern Nuba hills), SD-NO Northern (the Wadi al-Milk's lower course and the Kababish desert), SD-NW White Nile (the Umm Ruwaba–Kosti line).
- **Citation flags.** No pre-1821 Kordofan population total was retrieved (the 400–700k figure is a reconstruction flagged as such); El Obeid's pre-1883 population (50–100k) rests on recalled accounts of the siege, with 35,000 c. 1900 the one retrieved figure; the 1956 district totals are recalled, not retrieved; the 2024 population and all GDP shares are estimates.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 in Windows 1–2 (c. 4500–1300 BCE); band 9 only at the top of range c. 1650–1790; band 8 held 1983–2024 on the census-grade series.
