# SD-DW · West Darfur State (Wilayat Gharb Darfur), Sudan

**UNIT** SD-DW (ISO 3166-2) = West Darfur State. Scale flag (C14): an ADM1 unit of ~42,350 km² (the pre-2012 state had 79,460 km², of which Central Darfur took 37,114), six to nine times a NUTS-3 county; every share below carries the polygon-size caveat.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (since January 2012): ~42,350 km², seat El Geneina; localities El Geneina, Kereneik, Kulbus, Jebel Moon, Sirba, Habila, Foro Baranga, Beida. Dar Masalit on the Chad border, the Wadi Kaja and Wadi Azum lowlands and the Jebel Moon hills. Historic components: the Masalit sultanate (1880s–1956 as an autonomous dar), the marches between Darfur and Wadai, the condominium's Dar Masalit district and Darfur Province (1956–94), West Darfur State (1994) before the 2012 split. Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the African Humid Period savanna of the Wadi Kaja and Wadi Azum lowlands at the equivalence-rule densities.
**Documented-era peak** Window 16, 2018–2023: the Central Bureau of Statistics projection of 1,775,945 (2018) and OCHA's 1.9 million (2023), with El Geneina at 538,390 before the war; band 7 (census-grade on the CBS projection). The Masalit sultanate c. 1910 and the Darfur–Wadai marches never exceed band 7.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area ~42,350 km² of Sahelian plain and the Jebel Moon hills, with today's 400–700 mm of rain and the Wadi Kaja and Wadi Azum; wooded savanna in the African Humid Period.
2. Communities at the moment: Sahelian Neolithic herders and cultivators; Dar Masalit is archaeologically almost unexplored, so the equivalence rule for a non-literate society applies at the high end.
3. Density, equivalence rule, high end: 0.15–0.4 persons/km² on the savanna (Hassan's Saharan–Sahelian Neolithic range, C14); no highland core. → 6,300–16,800, reported 6.3–16.8k.
4. GDP multiplier 0.8–1.0 (pastoral-agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: European farmed-zone densities anywhere; killed a total above ~21,000 (>0.5/km² over the whole state).
- Survives: 16,800 people means ~85–170 camps and hamlets of 100 along the wadis; grazing and cropland need under 5% of the polygon.
- Allocation flag: no excavated site in the polygon anchors the figure.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across the humid-period Sahara and Sahel gives 1–2 million people against a 5M world floor, a fifth to two fifths of humanity.

## Flags
- **Scale (C14).** An ADM1 state of ~42,350 km²; at NUTS-3 scale its shares would be a sixth to a ninth. Every band above is a state-level band.
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is a Window 1 allocation on an unexcavated landscape. The documented-era record is census-grade only through the CBS projection and the OCHA profile; the 2008 current-territory figure is a derivation and the 2023 figure precedes the exodus. No pre-modern window exceeds band 7.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 (~7–8×). The contemporary figures are 1.0–1.5×.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-2012 figures are allocations from the pre-split state (Central Darfur's localities removed), from Darfur Province and from the sultanate marches; the current area is a derivation (79,460 less 37,114).
- **Double-count risk.** SD-DC Central Darfur (the 2008 parent state; the Azum valley), SD-DN North Darfur (Kabkabiya and the Zaghawa), Chad's Ouaddaï and Sila (Adré, the 2023 refugees, Wadai's eastern dars).
- **Citation flags.** The current area, the 2008 current-territory population and the GDP shares are estimates; no Masalit sultanate population figure was retrieved.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 7 on population 2018–2023; band 6 from 1993.
