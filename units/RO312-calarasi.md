# RO312 · Călărași County (Județul Călărași), Romania

**UNIT** RO312 (NUTS-3) = Călărași County.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (created 1981 from the southern half of Ialomița County and the eastern plăși of the former Ilfov; confirmed 1997): 5,088 km², seat Călărași; municipalities Călărași and Oltenița; towns Budești, Fundulea, Lehliu-Gară; 50 communes. The southern Bărăgan, the Mostiștea and lower Argeș–Dâmbovița valleys, the Danube terrace from Oltenița to Călărași and the southern part of the Ialomița Great Island (marsh until the 1960s drainage). Historic components: the southern plăși of the old Ialomița County (seat Călărași until 1952) and the Oltenița and Budești plăși of Ilfov County. 1950–1968 the territory sat in the Bucharest Region (polygon not used). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the Boian–Gumelnița transition on the Danube terrace and the Mostiștea, where both cultures have their type sites.
**Documented-era peak** Band 6 on a plateau from 1850 to 1956; population record 1948, GDP record 1912.
**WINNING PATHWAY** GDP in Window 1 (population tied); population in the documented era.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 5,088 km² (INS). The interior Bărăgan was steppe and the Great Island and Danube floodplain marsh, so the Chalcolithic settlement zone is the Danube terrace, the Mostiștea lakes, the Argeș–Dâmbovița confluence and the island levees: 3,200–4,000 km² usable.
2. Cultures at the moment: Boian (type site Lake Boian at Vărăști, inside the polygon) passing into Gumelnița (type site Măgura Gumelnița at Oltenița, inside the polygon); Sultana-Malu Roșu (tell 4500–3900 cal BC with the Boian flat settlement and cemeteries beside it), Căscioarele-Ostrovel (the sanctuary with the painted columns), Vlădiceasca, Radovanu, Chirnogi and Spanțov are the excavated anchors on the Mostiștea and the Danube.
3. Density 2–3 persons/km² on the usable area, the high end of Müller's (2013) regional Neolithic–Chalcolithic densities by the settlement-area method of Zimmermann, Hilpert & Wendt (2009). Equivalence rule applied. → 6,400–12,000, reported 6–12k.
4. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2 (default agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: farming densities on the Bărăgan steppe and the marsh (another 1,100–1,900 km²).
- Killed: any county total above ~16,000 (>4/km² usable).
- Survives: 12,000 people means ~60–120 tells and flat villages of 100–200 along the Danube, the Mostiștea and the Argeș at 4–7 km spacing, which is the attested Gumelnița tell density on the Mostiștea; cropland need under 3% of the usable area.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across Europe's farmed zone c. 4500 BCE gives a few million Europeans against a 5M world floor, a third to a half of humanity. Floor artefact shared with every farmed Old World unit.

## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 4.** The Danube facing Silistra (Ottoman fortress and 1853–54 siege, 1913 Cadrilater, events across the river); the Călărași–Silistra and Oltenița–Turtucaia crossings; the 1916 Turtucaia disaster is an event on the far bank. Nothing beyond a crossing of regional reach.
- **Mind 5.** The polygon holds the type sites of both the Boian and the Gumelnița cultures and the Căscioarele sanctuary with its painted columns, the reference landscape of the Lower Danube Chalcolithic; Sultana-Malu Roșu is the best-published Gumelnița tell. A nameable archaeological claim of regional rank, so 5.

## Flags
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is the Window 1 floor artefact; the usable-area choice (3,200–4,000 km² of 5,088) sets the extension band. The documented era is a flat band-6 plateau from 1850 to 1956 with no contested moment.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 only (~4–5×).
- **Boundary smearing.** The county dates from 1981; every pre-1930 figure is a back-projection from the 1930 current-territory total.
- **Double-count risk.** RO315 Ialomița (the old county's northern half and the Great Island's northern part), RO322 Ilfov and RO321 Bucharest (the old Ilfov plăși), RO317 Teleorman and RO314 Giurgiu (the Boian–Gumelnița landscape continues west). The Getic allocation must sum to ≤100% of Dacia across Romanian units.
- **Citation flags.** The 2022 GDP is a press figure; pre-1930 totals are reconstructions.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 6 held 1850–1956 on population and in 1912 on GDP.
