# SD-DC · Central Darfur State (Wilayat Wasat Darfur), Sudan

**UNIT** SD-DC (ISO 3166-2) = Central Darfur State. Scale flag (C14): a Sudanese state is an ADM1 unit of 25,000–350,000 km², five to ten times a NUTS-3 county; the user's instruction fixes the scale, and every share below carries the polygon-size caveat.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (state created January 2012 from West Darfur): 37,114 km², seat Zalingei; localities Zalingei, Azum, Wadi Salih (Garsila), Mukjar, Bindisi, Um Dukhun, Nertiti (West Jebel Marra), Rokero (North Jebel Marra). The western and southern slopes of Jebel Marra (3,042 m), the Wadi Azum and Wadi Salih drainage with the best-watered farmland in Darfur, and the Chad border plain at Um Dukhun. Historic components: the Fur heartland of the Keira sultanate (its early seat at Turra on Jebel Marra), the western Darfur districts of the Anglo-Egyptian condominium (Zalingei) and of Darfur Province (1956–94), then West Darfur State (1994–2012). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the African Humid Period, with Jebel Marra's slopes and the Wadi Azum and Wadi Salih carrying herding-and-farming communities at the equivalence-rule densities.
**Documented-era peak** Window 10, c. 1700 (Window 11, c. 1750, ties): the Keira sultanate's heartland with its seat at Turra and the Jebel Marra granaries, a quarter to a third of a sultanate lawyered at 0.8–1.3M; band 9 at the lawyered top (range 7–9; allocation-grade). Administrative-grade modern peak: the state government's 2018 survey of 2,499,000 people, band 8 (0.033%); the Central Bureau of Statistics projection gives band 6.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 37,114 km²; the Jebel Marra massif and its foot-slopes with 500–1,000 mm of rain and volcanic soils, 8,000–10,000 km², are the core; the Wadi Azum, Wadi Salih and Um Dukhun plains (27,000 km²) were savanna in the African Humid Period (c. 9000–3500 BCE), when Darfur's climate belts lay 300–500 km north of today's.
2. Communities at the moment: Saharan–Sahelian Neolithic herders and incipient cultivators (the Leiterband horizon of Wadi Howar lies to the north in North Darfur; Jebel Marra's own prehistory is barely excavated); the equivalence rule for a non-literate society applies at the high end.
3. Density, equivalence rule, high end: 0.8–1.5 persons/km² on the massif and its foot-slopes (the Sahel's best-watered highland, comparable to the Nile-free uplands of the Neolithic Sahara at their densest), 0.15–0.4 persons/km² on the savanna plains (Hassan's Saharan Neolithic range). → 10,500–25,800, reported 10.5–25.8k.
4. GDP multiplier 0.8–1.0 (pastoral-agrarian, below the European agrarian default).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: European farmed-zone densities (2–3/km²) anywhere in the polygon; killed a total above ~30,000 (>0.8/km² over the whole state).
- Survives: 25,800 people means ~130–260 camps and hamlets of 100 on the massif and along the wadis; grazing and cropland need under 5% of the polygon.
- Allocation flag: no excavated site in the polygon anchors the figure; it is an equivalence-rule density allocation.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across the humid-period Sahara and Sahel gives 1–2 million people against a 5M world floor, a fifth to two fifths of humanity; together with Europe's farmed zone at the same stacking the two regions would exceed the floor. The floor artefact is at its most extreme for large pastoral polygons.

## Deep score — Window 10, c. 1700 (documented-era peak; c. 1750 ties)

Numerator components
1. The Keira sultanate (c. 1650–1874) with its seat at Turra on Jebel Marra under Sulayman Solong's successors (Musa, Ahmad Bukr, Muhammad Dawra), before the court moved north of the massif (Shoba, c. 1750s) and to El Fasher (1791–92). Lawyered sultanate population 0.8–1.3M (the upper readings of the sultanate's size behind the 19th-century estimates; Browne's 200,000 of the 1790s is the low bound in credentialed print); the polygon's share 25–35% as the sultanate's agricultural core and royal seat → 200–450k.
2. GDP multiplier 0.7–1.1: Maddison's Africa ratio (~0.7 of world c. 1700) × role premium 1.0–1.6 relative to the region for the royal seat, the Jebel Marra granaries and the sultanate's trade (slaves, ivory, ostrich feathers and gum northward on the Darb al-Arba'in; by 1800 Darfur was Egypt's largest trading partner south of the Sahara), capped at 1.1 because the caravan terminus and the market towns (Kobbei) lay in North Darfur.

Denominator: 610M at 1700 (McEvedy & Jones; the Willcox interpolation of 582M would raise the shares by 5%).

Result: population 0.025–0.066% (band 7–9); GDP 0.017–0.072% (band 7–9; 0.037% at the mid-range of 300k × 0.75; 9 at the lawyered top). c. 1750 on Willcox's 694M with 200–500k → 0.029–0.072% population, 0.020–0.079% GDP (band 8–9), the court by then outside the polygon.

Veto notes
- Killed: a sultanate above 1.3M c. 1700 (Darfur had 1.33M in the 1955–56 census after two centuries of growth and the Mahdist losses); killed a polygon share above 35%.
- Killed: counting El Fasher, Kobbei and the Darb al-Arba'in terminus (North Darfur) or Dar Masalit (West Darfur).
- Allocation flag: the whole numerator is an allocation of an estimated total; adopted as the documented-era record because no built numerator exists for Darfur before the 1955–56 census, with the 2018 survey reported beside it.

## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 6.** Nameable but regional: Jebel Marra, the cradle and early seat of the Keira sultanate (Turra), the natural fortress of Darfur and since 2003 the redoubt of the Sudan Liberation Army (Abdul Wahid), never taken by Khartoum; the Wadi Salih–Zalingei corridor to Chad. No chokepoint beyond the region.
- **Mind 5.** Nameable but regional: the royal tombs and shrines of Turra; the Fur oral traditions of the Daju and Tunjur kingdoms; Zalingei's university. Nothing of national or world intellectual scale.

## Flags
- **Scale (C14).** An ADM1 state of 37,114 km²; at NUTS-3 scale its share would be a fifth to a tenth. Every band above is a state-level band.
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is a Window 1 density allocation on an unexcavated landscape. The documented-era record (c. 1700–1750) is an allocation of an estimated sultanate total and is band 9 only at the top of range, band 7–8 at the mid-range. The 2018 figure is a contested administrative survey; the census-grade series never exceeds band 6.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 (~6–7×); the c. 1700 GDP figure (~3×). The 2018 survey is ~2.4× the CBS projection.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-2012 figures are allocations from West Darfur (2008), Darfur Province (1956–93) and the sultanate; the sultanate's share is a judgement, not a count.
- **Double-count risk.** SD-DN North Darfur (El Fasher, Kobbei, Shoba, the Tunjur seats), SD-DW West Darfur (Dar Masalit; the 2008 parent state), SD-DS South Darfur (the southern Jebel Marra slopes and Nyala).
- **Citation flags.** No printed sultanate population total was retrieved (Browne's 200,000 via the 1810 Britannica is the only figure found); the 2008 locality figures were not retrieved; the GDP shares are estimates without state accounts.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 8–9 at the top of range c. 1650–1800 on population; band 8 in 2018 on the administrative count only; census-grade band 6 since 1993.
