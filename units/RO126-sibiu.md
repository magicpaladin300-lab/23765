# RO126 · Sibiu County (Județul Sibiu), Romania

**UNIT** RO126 (NUTS-3) = Sibiu County.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (Law 2/1968): 5,432 km², seat Sibiu; municipalities Sibiu, Mediaș; towns Agnita, Avrig, Cisnădie, Copșa Mică, Dumbrăveni, Miercurea Sibiului, Ocna Sibiului, Săliște, Tălmaciu; 53 communes. The Sibiu depression and the Mărginimea foothills under the Cindrel and Făgăraș mountains, the Hârtibaciu plateau, the Târnava Mare valley around Mediaș and the Olt gorge at Turnu Roșu. Historic components: the Saxon seats of Sibiu, Miercurea and Nocrich and the Two Seats of Mediaș and Șeica; Hungarian Szeben county (176,921 in 1910) plus the Mediaș, Agnita and Dumbrăveni districts of Nagy-Küküllő; the interwar counties of Sibiu (194,619 in 1930) and the western half of Târnava-Mare. Pre-1948 figures are allocations to the current territory; the INS current-territory series begins in 1948. Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: Petrești and Turdaș settlement of the Sibiu depression, the Târnava Mare and the Hârtibaciu valleys.
**Documented-era peak** Window 9, c. 1500: Sibiu (Hermannstadt), capital of the Saxon Universitas (1486), with its customs registers of 1500–97 recording the Wallachian and Oriental trade, and Mediaș; band 8 (9 at the very top; range 7–9). Census-grade peak: GDP band 8 (1850–1870, Sibiu as capital of Transylvania 1849–65), population band 7 (1787–1930).

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 5,432 km² (INS); the Cindrel, Lotru and Făgăraș mountains (~1,900 km²) are excluded: usable depression, plateau and valleys 3,200–3,600 km².
2. Cultures at the moment: Petrești (the type site lies just over the Alba border at Petrești) on the Sibiu depression and the Târnava Mare, late Turdaș on the Olt, with the Ocna Sibiului salt springs; Cucuteni–Ariușd contacts up the Olt.
3. Density 2–3 persons/km² on the usable area, the high end of Müller's (2013) regional Neolithic–Chalcolithic densities by the settlement-area method of Zimmermann, Hilpert & Wendt (2009). Equivalence rule applied. → 6,400–10,800, reported 6.4–10.8k.
4. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2 (default agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: farming densities on the Cindrel and Făgăraș; killed any county total above ~14,500 (>4/km² usable).
- Survives: 10,800 people means ~55–110 villages and hamlets of 100–200 along the Cibin, Hârtibaciu, Târnava Mare and Olt at 4–7 km spacing; cropland need under 3% of the usable area.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across Europe's farmed zone c. 4500 BCE gives a few million Europeans against a 5M world floor, a third to a half of humanity. Floor artefact shared with every farmed Old World unit.

Numerator components

## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 7.** Nameable command: Sibiu as capital of the Grand Principality of Transylvania (1692–1791 and 1849–65) and seat of the Saxon Universitas; the Turnu Roșu pass (Olt gorge), the main road from Transylvania to Wallachia, held by Sibiu's customs; the Sibiu arsenal (Conrad Haas, 1529–69); the Copșa Mică gas and the Mediaș gas-industry cluster; today's Sibiu automotive belt and airport on the A1. Not 8: a provincial capital on a secondary pass.
- **Mind 8.** Nameable claims of world scale: Hermann Oberth, born at Sibiu (1894), a founder of astronautics; Conrad Haas's Sibiu manuscript (1529–69), the earliest known description of multi-stage rockets; the Brukenthal Museum (1817), the first public museum in south-eastern Europe; the first book printed in Romanian (Sibiu, 1544, Filip Moldoveanul's Lutheran catechism); the 1376 guild statutes; Sibiu as European Capital of Culture (2007). A 9 is arguable on Oberth and Haas together; the peg stays 8 because neither claim founded a lasting school in the polygon.

## Flags
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is the Window 1 floor artefact. The documented-era record (c. 1500) is band 8 at the mid-range and band 9 only at the very top, resting on the register premium for towns that are an eighth to a sixth of the county; the census-grade plateau 1787–1910 is band 7–8 with 1850–1870 the highest tops (0.041%).
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 only (~5×); the c. 1500 figure is ~2.5×; the 1787–1910 figures are 1.2–1.7×.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-1948 figures rebuild the polygon from Szeben county plus the Mediaș, Agnita and Dumbrăveni districts of Nagy-Küküllő (±15k, 5%); the Hungarian district tables could not be fetched (mek.oszk.hu is blocked).
- **Double-count risk.** RO122 Brașov (the Universitas's other seats; the 1503 register's overlapping trade), RO121 Alba (Sebeș, the Petrești type site, the lower Târnava), RO125 Mureș (the Târnava Mare above Dumbrăveni), RO415 Vâlcea (the Olt gorge's southern end).
- **Citation flags.** The Sibiu registers' totals were not read (only their existence and the trade's character, from Pakucs-Willcocks); the 1787–1850 polygon totals are reconstructions; Sibiu's 1500 population (6–8k) is the literature's range.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 8 held c. 1480–1540 at the top of range and c. 1850–1870 on GDP; band 7 on population 1787–1930.
