# SD-KH · Khartoum State (Wilayat al-Khartum), Sudan

**UNIT** SD-KH (ISO 3166-2) = Khartoum State. Scale flag (C14): an ADM1 unit of 22,142 km², the smallest Sudanese state but still four to five times a NUTS-3 county; every share below carries the polygon-size caveat.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (state of 1994): 22,142 km², seat Khartoum; localities Khartoum, Jebel Awliya, Omdurman, Karari, Umbadda, Khartoum North (Bahri) and Sharg an-Nil. The confluence of the Blue and White Niles, the main Nile down to the Sabaloka gorge (Sixth Cataract), the Blue Nile up past Soba to the Gezira line and the White Nile up past Jebel Awliya. Historic components: the Khartoum Mesolithic and Neolithic type-sites (Khartoum Hospital, Shaheinab, Kadero, Geili), Soba (capital of Alodia, c. 500–c. 1500), the Abdallab seats Qerri and Halfaya, Turco-Egyptian Khartoum (founded 1821, capital from the 1820s), Mahdist Omdurman (1885–98), the condominium and republican capital, Khartoum Province (1956–73), Khartoum Region/Province (1974–94). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the Khartoum Neolithic (Shaheinab horizon) fisher-herder-cultivators of the confluence, the densest-known Neolithic site cluster of the Nilotic Sudan, at the equivalence-rule densities.
**Documented-era peak** Window 6, c. 900: Soba, capital of Alodia, "large and wealthy, with fine buildings, spacious dwellings and churches full of gold" (Ibn Selim al-Aswani, 10th century), with its Nile hinterland; band 10 at the lawyered top (range 8–10, Biraben's 226M floor). Robust documented-era peak: Window 16, 2018–2023, the capital's population at 0.105–0.119% of the world (CBS 2018 estimate 7,993,900; 9.0–9.6M on the eve of the April 2023 war), band 10 throughout, administrative-grade; the 2008 census (5,274,321, 0.0783%) is the gallery's highest true-census share. Contemporary GDP band 8–9 (PPP, estimated).

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 22,142 km²: the Blue Nile, White Nile and main Nile banks and terraces (~3,500 km²) and the semi-arid plain east and west of the rivers (~18,600 km²), savanna in the African Humid Period.
2. Communities at the moment: the Khartoum Neolithic (Shaheinab horizon, c. 4900–3800 BCE) on both banks of the main Nile and the confluence: Shaheinab (the type-site), Kadero (a settlement mound with a cemetery of over 200 graves), Geili, Zakiab, Umm Direiwa, Nofalab, Islang; herder-fisher-cultivators with domestic cattle and goats; the equivalence rule for a non-literate society applies at the high end.
3. Density, equivalence rule, high end: 1.5–2.5 persons/km² on the river strips (the confluence cluster is the densest Neolithic site concentration known in the Sudan; the upper figure reads the Kadero cemetery as a multi-century community of several hundred), 0.15–0.4 on the plain (Hassan's Saharan–Sahelian Neolithic range, C14). → 5.3–8.8k on the rivers, 2.8–7.4k on the plain, 8.0–16.2k in all.
4. GDP multiplier 0.8–1.1 (riverine herder-cultivators with cattle wealth; fine burnished and impressed pottery, imported Red Sea shell, amazonite and carnelian at Kadero).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: Nile-floodplain densities above 2.5/km² for a herder-fisher horizon; killed a total above ~20,000.
- Survives: 16,200 people means ~100–160 villages, camps and hamlets of 100–150, more than half on the rivers at 2–4 km spacing, consistent with the site density around Khartoum North and Omdurman.
- Allocation flag: the plain is an allocation; the river figure rests on the Khartoum Neolithic site lists (Arkell 1953; Krzyżaniak's Kadero; Caneva's Geili).

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking along the Nile and across the humid-period Sahel gives 2–3 million people against a 5M world floor, roughly half of humanity.

## Deep score — Window 6, c. 900 (documented-era peak, GDP)

Numerator components
1. Built numerator: Soba, capital of Alodia, on the Blue Nile 19 km above the confluence; the site covers 240–275 ha (Welsby 1991, 1998; the Polish survey of 2019–22), with churches, a palace complex, cemeteries and mud-brick quarters, of which about 1% has been excavated. At 80–150 persons/ha over the built core (half to two thirds of the site), 10–25k; al-Aswani's "large and wealthy" city lawyered to the top of that range. Welsby reads the archaeology as "a moderate centre": the consensus column.
2. Countryside: the Nile reaches from the Sabaloka to Soba and Jebel Awliya, Alodia's core with the Christian villages, the irrigated banks and the pastoral hinterland; 60–150k at 3–7/km² on the river strips plus the plain. Qerri and the northern reach included. State 80–180k.
3. GDP multiplier 1.3–1.9: Maddison's Africa ratio (~0.8 of world c. 900) × role premium 1.6–2.4 relative to the region for the capital of a kingdom al-Aswani ranked richer and larger than Makuria, with its gold ("the churches are full of gold"), its river trade with Makuria and Egypt and its Red Sea links. Capital premium argued (rubric 1.6–2.0 band, stacked only once per C12); no ledger, so the 2.0–2.5 band is not claimed.

Denominator: 226M at 900 (Biraben 1979; C2 deep floor); McEvedy & Jones's 240M would lower the shares by 6%.

Result: population 0.035–0.080% (band 8–9); GDP 0.046–0.151% (band 8–10; 0.0896% at the mid-range of 130k × 1.55, band 9; 10 at the lawyered top). Window 5, c. 550 on 185M (Soba in the century of Longinus's mission, 580): 60–150k × 1.2–1.7 → 0.039–0.138% GDP (band 8–10). Window 7, c. 1100 on Biraben's 301M: 80–180k × 1.2–1.7 → 0.032–0.102% GDP (band 8–10 at top).

Veto notes
- Killed: a Soba above 25,000 (the excavated density is low and the site includes cemeteries and open ground); killed a hinterland above 150k on a semi-arid reach; killed a multiplier above 1.9.
- Killed: counting Alodia's Gezira (SD-GZ) or the White Nile reaches above Jebel Awliya (SD-NW).
- Allocation flag: the countryside is an allocation; Soba anchors the town and the premium.

## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 8.** Nameable continental-scale claims: Omdurman, capital of the Mahdist state (1885–98), the largest indigenous polity of 19th-century Africa, and the siege of Khartoum (1884–85) and Battle of Omdurman (1898) that bracketed it; Khartoum as the capital of Africa's largest country (1956–2011) and the seat of the Khartoum Resolution (Arab League summit, 1967); Soba as a capital for a millennium; Turco-Egyptian Khartoum as the command point of the Upper Nile trade. Not 9: a national and Nilotic command, not a world one.
- **Mind 7.** Nameable claims: Arkell's Khartoum Hospital site ("Early Khartoum", 1949) and Shaheinab (1953), the type-sites that defined the Khartoum Mesolithic and Neolithic and the wavy-line pottery horizon of the Saharan–Sahelian world; Gordon Memorial College (1902) → University of Khartoum, the Sudan's intellectual centre; the Mahdiyya as a religious movement and Omdurman as its holy city (the Mahdi's tomb); the Sudan National Museum; Khartoum's modern Arabic literature and music. Not 8: no world-scale school or discovery.

## Flags
- **Scale (C14).** An ADM1 state of 22,142 km²; at NUTS-3 scale its all-time shares would be a quarter to a fifth, though the capital conurbation itself (Khartoum, Omdurman, Bahri) would carry most of the modern record into a NUTS-3-sized polygon.
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is a Window 1 allocation anchored on the Khartoum Neolithic type-sites. The documented-era record is band 10 two ways: robustly via the 2018–2023 population (administrative-grade, 1.0–1.1×), and at the lawyered top only via the c. 900 GDP figure (180k people, the 1.9× multiplier, Biraben's floor), which is band 9 at the mid-range. The 2023 figure precedes the war's displacement and is itself an estimate.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 (~5–7×); the c. 900 GDP figure (~3.5–4×); Omdurman c. 1895 (~3×). The modern figures are 1.0–1.1×.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-1956 figures are allocations from Alodia's core, the Abdallab lands and Khartoum Province; the Mahdist-era hinterland is a judgement share.
- **Double-count risk.** SD-GZ Gezira (Alodia's and the Funj's Gezira; the Khartoum reach of the Blue Nile), SD-NR River Nile (the Sabaloka and the Shendi reach), SD-NW White Nile (Jebel Awliya's upstream).
- **Citation flags.** No Alodian population total exists; Soba's population is read from the site area; the Omdurman figures above ~150,000 rest on the European captives' accounts (Ohrwalder, Slatin), not verified here; the 1956 and 1973 province totals are recalled, not retrieved; the 2023 population and the GDP shares are estimates; Khartoum's share of Sudan's GDP is an argued allocation (no published state accounts were found).
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 in Window 1 and, at the top of range only, c. 550–1100; band 10 held 2016–2023 on the administrative series; band 9 from the 1993 census to 2015.
