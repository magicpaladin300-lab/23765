# HU321 · Hajdú-Bihar county, Hungary

**UNIT** HU321 (NUTS-3) = Hajdú-Bihar county. **Polygon** current boundaries (1950 reform): 6,210.5 km², seat Debrecen. Formed from Hajdú county (3,343 km², 253,863 people in 1910 including Debrecen), the part of Bihar county left in Hungary after 1920 (seat Berettyóújfalu), and the Polgár–Egyek–Tiszacsege strip from Szabolcs. Divergences of a year or less ignored.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the Polgár island (Polgár-Csőszhalom, 67.5 ha Late Neolithic complex; Tiszapolgár-Basatanya, the Early Copper Age type site), the Herpály tell at Berettyóújfalu and the Hajdúság loess. **Documented-era peak** Window 12, c. 1900–1910.
**WINNING PATHWAY** GDP (population × 1.0–1.2); population practically tied.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE
Numerator: land split of 6,210 km²: Hajdúság loess, Polgár island and the Bihar plain (~3,700 km²) at 2–3 persons/km²; Sárrét and Berettyó floodplain margins (~1,500 km²) at 1–1.5; Hortobágy steppe and Nyírség sand (~1,000 km²) at 0.5–1. Pegs as HU332 (KRAP Early Copper Age hamlet pattern; Raczky's Öcsöd densities; Müller 2013; Zimmermann–Hilpert–Wendt 2009; equivalence-rule high end). The polygon contains the type site of the very horizon scored (Tiszapolgár-Basatanya) and the largest Late Neolithic complex of the Tisza region (Polgár-Csőszhalom, 67.5 ha, abandoned c. 4500 BCE). → 9,400–14,400 people. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2.
Denominator: 5.0M (rubric floors table). No lower published figure.
Vetoes: killed, counting Csőszhalom at tell-core density across 67.5 ha (its horizontal settlement was never simultaneously built up, per Raczky's excavations); killed, plain densities on the Hortobágy, a seasonally flooded steppe with sparse Copper Age settlement; killed, anything above ~16,000. Survives: 2–3/km² on the loess, matching the Polgár micro-region surveys where Late Neolithic and Early Copper Age sites lie 1–3 km apart.

## Deep score — c. 1900–1910 (documented-era peak)
Numerator: Hajdú county 253,863 (1910; ≈232,000 in 1900, Debrecen 75,006 → 92,729); Hungarian Bihar ≈170,000 (1910; ≈158,000 in 1900, scaled from the 1920 rump county); Szabolcs transfers ≈25,000 → present territory ≈408–418k (1900), ≈444–454k (1910). Cross-check: present-territory 1949 = 498,271. Denominator 1,564M (Maddison 2003) for 1900. Population 0.026% (band 7). GDP: Maddison Hungary 1900 at 1.33× world; county at 1.0–1.1× Hungary (Debrecen as the Kingdom's third city, railway and market hub, against the Hortobágy and Nyírség hinterland) → 1.33–1.46× → 0.035–0.039% (band 8).

## Candidate — Window 9, c. 1550 (Ruling A, argued)
Debrecen as the chief fair town of the Hungarian grey-cattle export (100,000–200,000 head a year to Vienna, Nuremberg and Venice in the mid-16th century, recorded in thirtieth-customs registers), with a town of 10–12k and a county-area population of 60–90k over 487M: 0.012–0.018% population; a commerce multiplier of 2–3× would give 0.025–0.055% GDP (band 7–9). Not adopted: the customs ledgers document the Kingdom's trade, not the polygon's value added.

## Candidate — Window 2, c. 1600 BCE
Otomani–Füzesabony tells of the Berettyó (Berettyóújfalu-Herpály, Bakonszeg-Kádárdomb, Gáborján-Csapszékpart) and Hajdúság; 2–4/km² on the prime zone → 10–20k over 36.2M = 0.028–0.055% population, 0.022–0.066% GDP → band 7–9. Not fully lawyered.

## Qualitative
- **Strategy 6.** Debrecen as the hub of the 16th–17th-century cattle export, the Hajdú towns as the Principality's military frontier settlements, the Hortobágy as Central Europe's largest grassland; 1849 and 1944–45 governments fall under the event-fame ban. No chokepoint or sole command.
- **Mind 6.** Reformed College of Debrecen (1538), the "Calvinist Rome" of Hungary; eponymous Tiszapolgár and Herpály cultures; the Hortobágy as a UNESCO cultural landscape.

## Flags
- Floor fragility as for HU332: on a 7M denominator the peak is 0.13–0.21%, still band 10.
- Thin lawyering (delta >3×) for windows 1–2.
- Boundary smearing: the Hungarian-Bihar and Szabolcs-strip reconstructions for 1900/1910 are mine; check against KSH's restated series.
- Double-count risk: the Berettyó–Sárrét prehistoric landscape continues into HU332 (Szeghalom); the Polgár strip is counted here and must not be counted under Szabolcs-Szatmár-Bereg (HU323).
- Ruling D: band 10 only in window 1; band 7–8 held c. 1850–1950.
