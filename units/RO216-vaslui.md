# RO216 · Vaslui County (Județul Vaslui), Romania

**UNIT** RO216 (NUTS-3) = Vaslui County.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (Law 2/1968): 5,318 km², seat Vaslui; municipalities Vaslui, Bârlad, Huși; towns Murgeni, Negrești; 81 communes. The Bârlad plateau and the Fălciu hills between the Bârlad and the Prut, all habitable hill country without mountains. Historic components: the interwar counties of Vaslui (2,260 km²; 139,503 in 1930), Fălciu (2,120 km², less its north-western communes now in Iași County) and the northern and central part of Tutova (Bârlad), less its southern communes now in Galați County; before 1859 the Moldavian districts of the same names. 1950–1968 the territory was split between the Iași and Bârlad regions (polygons not used). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**Documented-era peak** Window 11, 1850 (Willcox floor), band 7 at the lawyered top (range 6–7); the population share stays at 0.014–0.017% from 1850 to 1930.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 5,318 km² (INS), all plateau and hill country between 100 and 450 m; usable area taken as the whole polygon.
2. Cultures at the moment: Cucuteni A (c. 4600–4300 BCE), the heartland phase of the Cucuteni–Trypillia complex, with its settlements of large burnt houses and painted pottery on terrace spurs; the Vaslui plateau lies between the classic Cucuteni A clusters of the Iași and Bacău hills, and the county repertory records dozens of Cucuteni A and A–B findspots (Dumești, Rafaila, Bălțați, Gârceni, Poienești among the excavated ones).
3. Density 2–3 persons/km², the high end of Müller's (2013) regional Neolithic–Chalcolithic densities by the settlement-area method of Zimmermann, Hilpert & Wendt (2009), applied to the whole polygon. Equivalence rule applied. → 10,600–16,000, reported 10–16k.
4. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2 (default agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: applying the Trypillia giant-settlement densities of the Ukrainian forest-steppe (4100–3600 BCE, hundreds of hectares) to the Moldavian Cucuteni A, whose settlements are 1–5 ha.
- Killed: any county total above ~21,000 (>4/km²).
- Survives: 16,000 people means ~80–160 settlements of 100–200 at 5–8 km spacing across the Bârlad, Vaslui, Crasna and Prut valleys, consistent with the recorded site density; cropland need under 3% of the polygon.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across Europe's farmed zone c. 4500 BCE gives a few million Europeans against a 5M world floor, a third to a half of humanity. Floor artefact shared with every farmed Old World unit.

## Flags
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is the Window 1 floor artefact. The documented-era record is a trough-hunted 1850 whose band 7 holds only at the top of range; 1912 and 1930 give the same figure within rounding, so band 6–7 describes the whole 1850–1930 plateau.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 only (~4–5×).
- **Boundary smearing.** All figures before 1948 are reconstructions from three historic counties whose boundaries differ from the polygon by 10–20%.
- **Double-count risk.** RO213 Iași (the Fălciu remainder and the Cucuteni heartland), RO224 Galați (the Tutova remainder), RO211 Bacău (the Poienești–Lukaševka and Cucuteni A clusters west of the Bârlad).
- **Citation flags.** The 1850–1930 county totals on the current territory and the 2022 GDP are inferred from shares and per-capita ratios.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 6–7 held c. 1850–1930; band 6 population to 1992.
