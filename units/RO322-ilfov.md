# RO322 · Ilfov County (Județul Ilfov), Romania

**UNIT** RO322 (NUTS-3) = Ilfov County.
**Polygon** Current boundaries: 1,583 km² (the smallest county), the ring around Bucharest, which is excluded (RO321); 8 towns (Bragadiru, Buftea, Chitila, Măgurele, Otopeni, Pantelimon, Popești-Leordeni, Voluntari) and 32 communes on the Vlăsia plain, with the Snagov, Căldărușani and Colentina lake chains and the remnant Vlăsia forests. The polygon dates from 1981 (the Ilfov Agricultural Sector under Bucharest, made a county again in 1997); the historic Ilfov county (interwar 5,176 km²) held Bucharest and parts of today's Giurgiu, Călărași and Dâmbovița, and the 1968–81 county was larger still. Pre-1981 figures are allocations to the current territory. Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the Boian (Vidra phase)–Gumelnița tells of the Vlăsia plain (Vidra, Jilava, Glina, Măgurele, Chitila).
**Documented-era peak** Window 16, 2022–2023: GDP on the PPP basis (county accounts 2022 and 2023), band 6; population band 6 on the 1 January 2024 resident estimate (census-grade 2021: band 5). The second unit in the gallery whose documented-era record is contemporary.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 1,583 km² (INS); lakes and the wettest Colentina–Snagov bottoms excluded: usable plain 1,450–1,550 km². The whole polygon is Chalcolithic farmland; nothing is mountain.
2. Cultures at the moment: the Vidra phase of Boian passing into Gumelnița A1 on the Sabar, Argeș, Dâmbovița, Colentina and Snagov waters; tells at Vidra (the Vidra phase's type site, the "goddess of Vidra" vessel), Jilava, Glina, Măgurele, Chitila-Fermă and the lake-side sites of Snagov and Căldărușani. The polygon lies inside the Gumelnița heartland, the densest tell landscape of the Balkans.
3. Density 2–3 persons/km² on the usable area, the high end of Müller's (2013) regional Neolithic–Chalcolithic densities by the settlement-area method of Zimmermann, Hilpert & Wendt (2009), as for Călărași and Giurgiu. Equivalence rule applied. → 2,900–4,650, reported 3–4.6k.
4. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2 (default agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: any county total above ~6,200 (>4/km² usable); killed a "tell heartland" density above the Călărași–Giurgiu figure, since the Ilfov tells are the same 1–3 ha sites.
- Survives: 4,600 people means ~25–45 tells and flat villages of 100–200 at 5–8 km spacing along the Sabar, Argeș, Colentina and the lake chain; cropland need under 3% of the usable area.
- Band 10 holds only at the top of the range (0.093% population, 0.112% GDP); the mid-range is band 9.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across Europe's farmed zone c. 4500 BCE gives a few million Europeans against a 5M world floor, a third to a half of humanity. Floor artefact shared with every farmed Old World unit.

## Deep score — Window 16, 2022–2023 (documented-era peak)

Numerator components
1. GDP: Ilfov produced 33,270 million lei in 2022 (INS regional accounts; 2.37% of Romania's 1,401,345 million lei) and 45,666 million lei in 2023 (INS via OECD regional statistics; 2.85% of 1,604,554 million lei), the fastest-rising county share in the country (logistics and retail belts at Chiajna, Afumați and Ștefănești, the Pipera–Voluntari offices, the Otopeni airport zone, the Mioveni-free industrial ring). County accounts are production-based, so the output of Bucharest commuters working in Ilfov is already inside the figure and Ilfov residents working in Bucharest are outside it (C8).
2. Denominators on the same basis and vintage for 2022: $100.9T nominal and $164.2T PPP (World Bank WDI, July 2023 vintage); Romania $808.2B PPP; 4.6885 lei/$. Nominal 2022: $7.10B → 0.0070%. PPP 2022: 2.374% × $808.2B = $19.2B → 0.0117%.
3. 2023 at the lawyered top: the 2.846% county share carried on Romania's 2022 share of world PPP output (0.492%) gives 0.0140% PPP; on the nominal basis (World Bank 2023: Romania $351B, world ~$105.4T) 0.0095%. The 2023 PPP pairing is a ratio carry, not a same-vintage pairing, and is flagged below.

Result: GDP 0.0070–0.0095% nominal (band 6); 0.0117–0.0140% PPP (band 6). Basis flag: PPP (C5).

Population: 542,686 at the 2021 census over 7,886M → 0.0069% (band 5, a hair under the 0.007% line); the INS resident-population estimate of 583,547 at 1 January 2024 (TEMPO; 545,407 in 2022) over 8,114–8,160M → 0.0072% (band 6). Ilfov is the only county whose population grew 2011–2021 (+40%) and the only one with positive natural increase in 2024.

Veto notes
- Killed: the Bucharest–Ilfov region's figures (27–30% of national GDP), which are not this polygon.
- Killed: a 2023 same-vintage pairing, which was not retrieved; the ratio carry cannot move the band (band 7 would need a county share of ~3.05% at Romania's world-PPP share).

## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 6.** Nameable but national-scale: Henri Coandă airport at Otopeni, Romania's main gateway (civil from 1965–70), the Bucharest ring road and the A0 orbital, the Bucharest–Giurgiu line of 1869 (Romania's first railway) across the polygon; no continental chokepoint. Snagov as the party leadership's retreat is event fame, not command.
- **Mind 8.** Nameable claims: the twin 10 PW lasers of ELI-NP at Măgurele, the world's most powerful laser since March 2019 (Guinness; Thales–ELI-NP); the Măgurele Institute of Atomic Physics (1949–56), home of Romania's first computer CIFA-1 (1957) and research reactor (1957); the Snagov press of Antim Ivireanul, where the first Greek–Arabic Liturgikon was printed in 1701 and whose equipment seeded the first Arabic-type press of the Ottoman Empire at Aleppo (1706); Brâncoveanu's Mogoșoaia palace (1702), the type specimen of the Brâncovenesc style. A world-record instrument and a first in printing carry the peg to 8; Strategy stays 6.

## Flags
- **Ambiguity.** Band 10 holds only at the top of the Window 1 range (maximum usable area and the 3/km² density together); the mid-range is band 9. The documented-era record is contemporary and band 6 on every reading; no earlier window reaches band 7.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 (~3–4×). The contemporary figures are 1.0–1.2× consensus.
- **Pairing flag.** The 2023 PPP figure (0.0140%) carries the 2023 county share on Romania's 2022 share of world PPP output; the fully paired 2022 figure is 0.0117%. The 2024 population uses an INS estimate, not a census.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-1948 figures are allocations from the much larger historic Ilfov county (±10–15%); the INS current-territory series folds 1930 Ilfov into Bucharest.
- **Double-count risk.** RO321 Bucharest (the suburban belt and commuters, kept apart by the production basis), RO314 Giurgiu (Popești and the Vidra-phase sites across the Argeș), RO312 Călărași (the Gumelnița heartland).
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1 and only at the top of range; band 6 held 2022–2024 on GDP and 2024 on population.
