# SD-NB · Blue Nile State (Wilayat an-Nil al-Azraq), Sudan

**UNIT** SD-NB (ISO 3166-2) = Blue Nile State. Scale flag (C14): an ADM1 unit of 45,844 km², six to ten times a NUTS-3 county; every share below carries the polygon-size caveat.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (state of 1994): 45,844 km², seat Ed Damazin; localities Ed Damazin, Roseires, Bau, Geisan, Kurmuk, Tadamon, Wad al-Mahi. The upper Blue Nile from the Ethiopian border to below Roseires, the Ingessana hills, and the wooded savanna of the Ethiopian foothills with 600–1,000 mm of rain. Historic components: the kingdom of Fazughli and the Funj sultanate's gold-bearing southern province, the Turco-Egyptian Fazoghli district (1821–), the condominium's Fung Province (Blue Nile Province), Blue Nile Province of 1956–74 and Central Region (1974–94). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the African Humid Period woodland savanna and the Blue Nile valley at the equivalence-rule densities.
**Documented-era peak** Window 16, 2024: the Central Bureau of Statistics projection of 1,108,391 (2018) carried to 1.25–1.40M in 2024 with the displaced of the 2023– war; band 7 at the top of range (census-grade on the CBS projection; 2018 itself sits a hair below the band-7 line). Allocation-grade candidate: the Funj–Fazughli gold province c. 1700 (band 5–7), not adopted.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 45,844 km²; the Blue Nile valley and its terraces (~5,000 km²) and the woodland savanna of the Ingessana hills and the Ethiopian foothills (~40,000 km²), the best-watered part of northern Sudan, wetter still in the African Humid Period.
2. Communities at the moment: riverine fisher-herder-cultivators of the Blue Nile (the Khartoum Neolithic tradition reaches up the river) and savanna herders; the polygon is archaeologically little explored, so the equivalence rule for a non-literate society applies at the high end.
3. Density, equivalence rule, high end: 0.5–1.0 persons/km² on the river strip, 0.15–0.4 persons/km² on the savanna (Hassan's Saharan–Sahelian Neolithic range, C14). → 8,500–21,000, reported 8.5–21k.
4. GDP multiplier 0.8–1.0 (pastoral-agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: European farmed-zone densities anywhere; killed a total above ~25,000 (>0.55/km² over the whole state).
- Survives: 21,000 people means ~100–210 camps and hamlets of 100 along the river and the hill wadis; grazing and cropland need under 5% of the polygon.
- Allocation flag: no excavated site in the polygon anchors the figure.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across the humid-period Sahara and Sahel gives 1–2 million people against a 5M world floor, a fifth to two fifths of humanity; with Europe's farmed zone at the same stacking the two regions exceed the floor. The floor artefact is at its most extreme for large pastoral polygons.

## Flags
- **Scale (C14).** An ADM1 state of 45,844 km²; at NUTS-3 scale its shares would be a sixth to a tenth. Every band above is a state-level band.
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is a Window 1 density allocation on an unexcavated landscape. The documented-era record reaches band 7 only on the 2024 trend estimate; the census-grade 2008 and 2018 figures are band 6 (2018 at 0.0146%, a hair under the line).
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 (~7–8×); the Funj–Fazughli figures (~3×). The contemporary figures are 1.0–1.15×.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-1994 figures are allocations from Blue Nile Province and Central Region; the Funj-era figures are allocations of a province without counts.
- **Double-count risk.** SD-SI Sennar (the Funj core and the Blue Nile below Roseires), Ethiopia's Benishangul-Gumuz (Beni Shangul's gold, the Fazughli kingdom's eastern half), South Sudan's Upper Nile (the Kurmuk–Maban border).
- **Citation flags.** Wikipedia dates the 832,112 figure to a "2006 census"; it is the 2008 census figure. The 2024 population and the GDP shares are estimates; no Fazughli gold output or Funj population total was retrieved.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 7 on population only on the 2024 trend; band 6 from the 1970s.
