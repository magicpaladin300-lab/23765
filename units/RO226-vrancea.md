# RO226 · Vrancea County (Județul Vrancea), Romania

**UNIT** RO226 (NUTS-3) = Vrancea County.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (Law 2/1968): 4,857 km², seat Focșani; municipalities Focșani and Adjud; towns Mărășești, Odobești, Panciu; 68 communes. The Vrancea Mountains in the west (~35%), the Subcarpathian Vrancea depression and the Odobești–Panciu vine hills, and the Siret plain in the east. Historic components: the interwar Putna County (seat Focșani) with the Adjud area, plus the northern plăși of Râmnicu Sărat County (Dumitrești, Chiojdeni) and the Siret-bank communes of Tecuci. 1950–1968 the territory sat in the Galați and Bacău regions (polygons not used). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: Cucuteni–Stoicani-Aldeni settlement of the Siret plain and the Subcarpathian valleys.
**Documented-era peak** Window 11, 1850 (Willcox floor): band 7 at the lawyered top (range 6–7); population band 6 from 1912 to 1966.
**WINNING PATHWAY** GDP in Window 1 (population tied); both pathways level in the documented era.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 4,857 km² (INS); the mountain third is excluded: usable Siret plain, Subcarpathian depression and vine hills 3,000–3,300 km².
2. Cultures at the moment: the Cucuteni–Gumelnița contact zone, the Stoicani–Aldeni aspect of the lower Siret with Cucuteni A to the north; the Siret terraces (Mândrișca, Cândești, Bonțești) and the Vrancea depression carry the findspots.
3. Density 2–3 persons/km² on the usable area, the high end of Müller's (2013) regional Neolithic–Chalcolithic densities by the settlement-area method of Zimmermann, Hilpert & Wendt (2009). Equivalence rule applied. → 6,000–9,900, reported 6–10k.
4. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2 (default agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: farming densities on the mountain third; killed any county total above ~13,000 (>4/km² usable).
- Survives: 10,000 people means ~50–100 villages and hamlets of 100–200 along the Siret, Putna, Milcov and Râmna at 5–8 km spacing; cropland need under 3% of the usable area.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across Europe's farmed zone c. 4500 BCE gives a few million Europeans against a 5M world floor, a third to a half of humanity. Floor artefact shared with every farmed Old World unit.

## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 5.** The Milcov border between Moldavia and Wallachia at Focșani, the Siret corridor and the Adjud junction; the Mărășești–Mărăști–Oituz battles of 1917 are events. Nothing of more than regional reach.
- **Mind 5.** The Țara Vrancei as the archetype of the Romanian free-peasant "republic" and the claimed homeland of the *Miorița* ballad; the Mărășești Mausoleum as a national memory site; Vârteșcoiu as a type site; the Odobești–Panciu wine region, among Romania's largest. Nothing nameable above national rank.

## Flags
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is the Window 1 floor artefact. The documented-era record reaches band 7 only at the top of the 1850 range; the midpoint is band 6, and the population pathway is a flat band 6 from 1912 to 1966.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 only (~4–5×).
- **Boundary smearing.** The polygon unites Putna with Râmnicu Sărat and Tecuci margins; pre-1948 figures are reconstructions (±10k).
- **Double-count risk.** RO222 Buzău (the Râmnicu Sărat remainder), RO224 Galați (the Siret bank and Tecuci), RO211 Bacău (Adjud's hinterland and the Carpi heartland), RO123 Covasna (the Vrancea Mountains' far side).
- **Citation flags.** The 1930 total on the current territory is inferred.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 6–7 held c. 1850–1912; band 6 population held 1912–1992.
