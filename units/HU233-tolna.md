# HU233 · Tolna county, Hungary

**UNIT** HU233 (NUTS-3) = Tolna county. **Polygon** current boundaries: 3,703 km², seat Szekszárd. The historic county (3,643 km², about 267,000 people in 1910) is nearly identical; 1950 adjustments were marginal. Divergences of a year or less ignored.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: Late Lengyel dispersal across the Tolna loess hills and the Sárköz after the collapse of the Alsónyék mega-site. **Documented-era peak** Window 11, c. 1870.
**WINNING PATHWAY** GDP (population × 1.0–1.2); population practically tied.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE
Numerator: all-lowland and loess-hill polygon of 3,703 km² at 2–3 persons/km² (pegs as HU332, transposed to the Transdanubian Lengyel world: the eponymous Lengyel-Sánc, Mórágy-Tűzkődomb, and the Alsónyék complex of ~80 ha with 122 post-framed houses and ~2,300 Lengyel burials, Bánffy, Osztás, Oross, Zalai-Gaál et al.; Müller 2013; Zimmermann–Hilpert–Wendt 2009; equivalence-rule high end), plus a residual 0–300 at Alsónyék, whose burials run to 4585–4515 cal BC. → 7,400–11,400 people. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2.
Denominator: 5.0M (rubric floors table). No lower published figure.
Vetoes: killed, counting Alsónyék at its boom size: the settlement ended 4670–4565 cal BC and its peak lasted a generation or two around 4700 cal BC, before the window opens; killed, anything above ~12,000 (>3.2/km²). Survives: 2–3/km² matches the dense Late Lengyel site record of the Tolna hills and the Sárköz revealed by the M6 motorway excavations.

## Deep score — c. 1870 (documented-era peak)
Numerator: present-territory population ≈218–224k in 1870 (historic county 1869 census ≈220k on a near-identical area); 1900 ≈250–255k; 1910 ≈263–268k. Cross-check: present-territory 1980 = 266,273. Denominator 1,272M (Maddison) for 1870. Population 0.017% (band 7). GDP: Maddison Hungary 1870 at 1.26× world; county at 1.0–1.15× Hungary (Szekszárd wine, the German-settled Schwäbische Türkei farms, Danube trade at Paks and Tolna) → 1.26–1.45× → 0.022–0.025% (band 7). 1900 and 1910 give 0.016% and 0.015% population, 0.021–0.023% GDP, the same band.

## Candidate — Window 2, c. 1600 BCE
Vatya fortified sites of the Mezőföld edge (Bölcske-Vörösgyír, Paks-Sánchegy) and Transdanubian Encrusted Pottery settlements; 2–4/km² → 7,400–14,800 over 36.2M = 0.020–0.041% population, 0.016–0.049% GDP → band 7–8. Not fully lawyered.

## Qualitative
- **Strategy 7.** Paks Nuclear Power Plant (four VVER-440 units, about half of Hungary's electricity since the late 1980s, the country's only nuclear plant; Paks II under construction). A nameable installation of national scale, below the Suez peg of 9.
- **Mind 5.** Eponymous Lengyel culture (Wosinsky's excavations of the 1880s); Alsónyék as the largest Lengyel site known; Szekszárd abbey (1061, burial place of Béla I); Cikádor at Bátaszék, the first Cistercian abbey in Hungary (1142).

## Anchor comparisons
All-time peak (0.15–0.27%): overlaps HU333 (0.17–0.31%) and Potosí c. 1600 (R4, 0.15–0.2% GDP), below HU332 and Angkor. Documented-era peak (0.022–0.025%): above Cahokia (R6), below HU332's 1900 GDP.
