## Deep score — Window 16, 2022 (record) and Window 13, 1938 (tie)

Numerator components, 1938 (post-1820, so the Maddison series per the rubric)
1. Romania 1938: 19.75M people × $1,242 per head (Maddison 2003) = $24.5B (1990 international dollars), 0.573% of a $4,281B world.
2. Bucharest's share of national product 15–22%: the city held about 4% of the population but roughly a fifth of industrial output, the banks, the ministries and most of the service economy of a country whose industry had come to equal agriculture in national income by 1938. Consensus 12–14%.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE (for the record)
The polygon's ≈240 km² at the high-end density of 2–3 persons/km² (Müller 2013; equivalence rule) gives 480–720 people at the Boian–Gumelnița transition; Bucharest holds the eponymous sites of the Dudești culture and the Boian–Giulești phase and a score of Neolithic findspots along the Dâmbovița and Colentina. ## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 8.** Nameable command: capital of the United Principalities from 1862 and of Romania since, seat of government and general staff for a state of 7–23 million; Wallachia's capital from 1659; the 1877–78, 1916–18 and 1944 campaigns turned on it (events, not scored). A national command, not a global chokepoint, so 8.
- **Mind 8.** Nameable institution: the seat of the Romanian Patriarchate (1925), the second-largest autocephalous Orthodox church by membership; the University (1864), the Romanian Academy (1866), the Athenaeum (1888) and the interwar "Little Paris" of Eliade, Cioran, Ionesco and Enescu's circle as the national cultural capital. The 1989 revolution and the 1977 earthquake fall under the event-fame ban.

On the nominal basis 2022 is band 9. Earliest attainment of band 10: 1912 (top of range); sustained from the 1930s.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** None in the documented era; Window 1 only (~4×), and it is not the record.
- **Boundary smearing.** None material: the INS series is on the current territory from 1912; the 1938 population is interpolated.
- **Double-count risk.** RO322 Ilfov (the Bucharest–Ilfov region's GDP and the 1981–1997 Agricultural Sector; commuters are counted where they work, i.e. here); the Prahova and Dâmbovița oil refined or traded through Bucharest firms is credited to the producing counties.
- **Basis flag.** PPP pairing (World Bank WDI, July 2023 vintage) for 2022; IMF pairing gives ~0.11%.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 (GDP) touched from 1912 and held from the 1930s to the present on the lawyered top; band 8 population 1948–1992.
