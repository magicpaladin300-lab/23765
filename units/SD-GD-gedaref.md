# SD-GD · Gedaref State (Wilayat al-Qadarif), Sudan

**UNIT** SD-GD (ISO 3166-2) = Gedaref (Al Qadarif) State. Scale flag (C14): an ADM1 unit of 75,263 km², ten to fifteen times a NUTS-3 county; every share below carries the polygon-size caveat.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (state of 1994, unchanged in substance since): 75,263 km², seat Gedaref; localities Gedaref town, Central Gedaref, Western and Eastern Galabat, Al-Fashaga, Al-Faw, Al-Rahad, Butana, Al-Mafaza, Al-Qureisha, Basunda, Al-Showak. The southern Butana and the eastern clay plains between the Atbara, the Rahad and the Dinder, the Gallabat gate to Ethiopia and the disputed Fashaga triangle. Historic components: the eastern marches of Meroe's Island (the Butana) and of the Funj sultanate, the Shukriya nomad dar, the condominium's Kassala Province (Gedaref district), Kassala Province of 1956–74, Eastern Region (1974–94). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the African Humid Period savanna of the Butana and the Atbara–Rahad plains at the equivalence-rule densities.
**Documented-era peak** Window 16, 2018–2024: the Central Bureau of Statistics projection of 2,208,385 (2018) carried to 2.5–2.8M in 2024, on the mechanized-sorghum and sesame economy of Sudan's granary; band 8 (census-grade on the CBS projection). Allocation-grade candidate: the Meroitic Butana c. 100 CE (band 6–9 at the top), not adopted.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 75,263 km² of clay plain and the southern Butana, today's 400–800 mm belt, wooded savanna in the African Humid Period (c. 9000–3500 BCE) with the Atbara, Rahad and Dinder as perennial or near-perennial rivers.
2. Communities at the moment: the Butana Group and the Khashm el-Girba Neolithic of the Atbara (the type sites lie just across the river in Kassala), herders and cultivators with sorghum gathering and early domestication; the equivalence rule for a non-literate society applies at the high end.
3. Density, equivalence rule, high end: 0.15–0.4 persons/km² on the savanna (Hassan's Saharan–Sahelian Neolithic range); no highland core (C14). → 11,300–30,100, reported 11.3–30k.
4. GDP multiplier 0.8–1.0 (pastoral-agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: European farmed-zone densities anywhere; killed a total above ~38,000 (>0.5/km² over the whole state).
- Survives: 30,000 people means ~150–300 camps and hamlets of 100 along the Atbara, Rahad, Dinder and the Butana wadis; grazing and cropland need under 5% of the polygon.
- Allocation flag: the Butana Group sites are mostly across the Atbara; the polygon's own figure is an equivalence-rule allocation.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across the humid-period Sahara and Sahel gives 1–2 million people against a 5M world floor, a fifth to two fifths of humanity; with Europe's farmed zone at the same stacking the two regions exceed the floor. The floor artefact is at its most extreme for large pastoral polygons.

## Flags
- **Scale (C14).** An ADM1 state of 75,263 km²; at NUTS-3 scale its shares would be a tenth to a fifteenth. Every band above is a state-level band.
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is a Window 1 density allocation on a thinly excavated landscape. The documented-era record is census-grade only through the CBS projection (the last census is 2008); the Meroitic candidate would reach band 9 at the top but has no built numerator inside the polygon.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 (~7–8×); the Meroitic and Funj figures (~3×). The contemporary figures are 1.0–1.1×.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-1994 figures are allocations from Kassala Province and Eastern Region; the Fashaga triangle's population is counted as Sudanese.
- **Double-count risk.** SD-KA Kassala (the Atbara's left bank, Khashm el-Girba and the Butana Group type sites), SD-NR River Nile (Meroe, Naqa, Musawwarat), SD-SI Sennar and SD-GZ Gezira (the Rahad and Dinder lower courses, the Funj core), Ethiopia's Amhara and Tigray (Metemma, the Fashaga).
- **Citation flags.** The 2024 population and the GDP shares are estimates; no Meroitic or Funj population total for the eastern marches was retrieved.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 8 on population from c. 2016 on the CBS trend; band 7 from the 1980s.
