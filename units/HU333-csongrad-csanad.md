# HU333 · Csongrád-Csanád county, Hungary

**UNIT** HU333 (NUTS-3) = Csongrád-Csanád county (Csongrád until 2020). **Polygon** current boundaries (1950 reform): 4,263 km², seat Szeged. The historic Csongrád county (3,569 km², 325,568 people in 1910 including the municipal towns Szeged and Hódmezővásárhely) gained the Makó district of Csanád county (Makó, Kiszombor, Apátfalva, Magyarcsanád, Csanádpalota, Pitvaros, Földeák, Maroslele and smaller villages) in 1950. Divergences of a year or less ignored.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the southern Tisza-culture core (Hódmezővásárhely-Gorzsa, Kökénydomb, Szegvár-Tűzköves, Tápé-Lebő) passing into the Tiszapolgár horizon. **Documented-era peak** Window 12, c. 1900–1910.
**WINNING PATHWAY** GDP (population × 1.0–1.2); population practically tied.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE
Numerator: all-lowland polygon of 4,263 km² at 2–3 persons/km² (same pegs as HU332: KRAP Early Copper Age hamlet pattern; Raczky's Öcsöd-Kováshalom densities of 136–204 persons/ha in the core and 230–250 people on 3 ha; Müller 2013 and Zimmermann–Hilpert–Wendt 2009 regional densities; equivalence-rule high end). The county holds the densest Late Neolithic tell cluster of the southern Alföld: Gorzsa (3–3.5 ha tell), Kökénydomb, Szegvár-Tűzköves, Tápé-Lebő, with Early Copper Age Tiszapolgár sites at Deszk and Hódmezővásárhely-Bodzáspart. → 8,500–12,800 people. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2.
Denominator: 5.0M (rubric floors table). No lower published figure.
Vetoes: killed, treating the Gorzsa and Szegvár tells as fully occupied at tell-core density in 4500 BCE (their late Tisza phases end c. 4500–4400 cal BC and the radiocarbon record shows contraction); killed, any figure above ~15,000 (>3.5/km²). Survives: 3/km² is consistent with the Hódmezővásárhely and Szentes district site inventories, which record Körös, Szakálhát, Tisza and Tiszapolgár sites at 2–4 km spacing along the Tisza and Maros levees.

## Deep score — c. 1900–1910 (documented-era peak)
Numerator: historic Csongrád county 325,568 (1910; about 296,000 in 1900, with Szeged 102,991 and Hódmezővásárhely 60,824) plus the Makó district of Csanád (Makó 34,918 in 1910 and its villages, ≈60,000; ≈56,000 in 1900) → present territory ≈345–355k (1900), ≈380–390k (1910). Cross-check: present-territory 1980 = 456,300. Denominator 1,564M (Maddison 2003) for 1900, 1,739M for 1910. Population 0.022% (band 7). GDP: Maddison Hungary 1900 at 1.33× world, 1910 at 1.38×; the county at 1.05–1.2× Hungary (Szeged the Kingdom's fourth city: river port, hemp and paprika processing, Pick salami from 1869, railway hub; Hódmezővásárhely and Makó market towns) → 1.40–1.65× world → 0.031–0.036% (band 8).

## Candidate — Window 2, c. 1600 BCE
Maros (Perjámos) culture cemeteries and settlements of the lower Maros (Szőreg C, Deszk A and F, Ószentiván, Pitvaros, Kiszombor), 2–4/km² → 8,500–17,000 over 36.2M = 0.024–0.047% population, 0.019–0.056% GDP → band 7–9. Not fully lawyered.

## Qualitative
- **Strategy 6.** Szeged as the royal salt chamber of medieval Hungary, the depot where Transylvanian salt shipped down the Maros was distributed under the crown monopoly (a nameable commodity hub, national rather than global, so below 8); Ottoman sanjak of Szeged 1543–1686; Tisza–Maros confluence and river port; the 1849 and 1919 governments at Szeged fall under the event-fame ban.
- **Mind 6.** University of Szeged, where Szent-Györgyi isolated vitamin C in quantity from paprika (Nobel 1937); the ELI-ALPS attosecond laser facility; Szegvár-Tűzköves "Sickle God" and the Kökénydomb Venus as icons of Neolithic art; Banner's definition of the Körös culture from Hódmezővásárhely sites; Ópusztaszer as the legendary first assembly of the conquering Magyars (Anonymus; legend, not structure).

## Flags
- Floor fragility as for HU332: on a 7M denominator the peak is 0.12–0.18%, still band 10.
- Thin lawyering (delta >3×) for windows 1–2.
- Boundary smearing: the Makó-district addition (≈56–60k) is my reconstruction; check against KSH's restated series. 1949–1970 present-territory figures not retrieved.
- Double-count risk: the Makó district is counted here and must not be counted under HU332; the northern Csanád villages were counted under HU332 and are excluded here.
- The 2022 county GDP is an estimate (about 3.1% of national GDP); replace with the KSH figure when available.
- Ruling D: band 10 only in window 1; band 7–8 held c. 1850–1950.
