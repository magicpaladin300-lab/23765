# HU312 · Heves county (Heves vármegye), Hungary

**UNIT** HU312 (NUTS-3) = Heves county. **Polygon** current boundaries (1950 reform): 3,637 km², seat Eger. The historic county (3,761 km², 279,700 people in 1910) lost the Tiszafüred district east of the Tisza to Jász-Nagykun-Szolnok and the Pásztó area to Nógrád, and gained the Bélapátfalva area north of Eger from Borsod. Divergences of a year or less ignored.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE, Tiszapolgár horizon on the Heves plain and the Mátra–Bükk foothills. **Documented-era peak** Window 11–12, c. 1870–1910.
**WINNING PATHWAY** GDP (population × 1.0–1.2); population practically tied.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE
Numerator: land split of the polygon into a prime early-farming zone (Hevesi-sík, Tisza floodplain, Gyöngyös–Hatvan plain, Mátraalja and Bükkalja foothills, ~2,300 km²) at 2–3 persons/km² (same pegs as HU332: KRAP Early Copper Age hamlet pattern, Raczky's Öcsöd-Kováshalom densities, Müller 2013 and Zimmermann–Hilpert–Wendt 2009 regional densities, equivalence-rule high end), the Heves–Borsod hills (~500 km²) at 1–1.5, and the Mátra and western Bükk (~800 km²) at 0.2–0.5. → 5,300–8,000 people. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2 (default agrarian).
Denominator: 5.0M (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE). No lower published figure.
Vetoes: killed, applying plain densities to the Mátra interior above 500 m (no Copper Age settlement there); killed, anything above ~9,500 (>2.6/km² county-wide), which would exceed the all-lowland HU332 rate on a one-quarter mountainous polygon. Survives: 2–3/km² on the plain and foothills matches the dense Alföld Linear Pottery to Tiszapolgár site record of the Bükkalja and Heves plain.

## Deep score — c. 1900 (documented-era peak)
Numerator: historic county 255,345 (1900) and 279,700 (1910); minus the Tiszafüred district (~30k) and Pásztó area (~20k), plus the Bélapátfalva area (~12k): present territory ≈218–224k (1900), ≈242k (1910). Cross-check: KSH present-territory 1949 = 316,273. Denominator 1,564M (Maddison 2003). Population 0.014% (band 6). GDP: Maddison Hungary 1900 at 1.33× world; Heves relative to Hungary 1.0–1.1 (Eger wine district, Hatvan sugar refinery of 1889, Gyöngyös market town; Demeter–Pénzes 1910 indices around the national average) → 0.018–0.021% (band 7). 1870 gives the same bands (178–184k over 1,272M: 0.014%; GDP 0.018–0.021%).

## Candidate — Window 2, c. 1600 BCE
Eponymous Hatvan culture (Hatvan-Strázsahegy) and Füzesabony culture (Füzesabony-Öregdomb) tells, with Szihalom-Földvár and Poroszló-Aponhát. 3–5/km² on the prime zone, 1 elsewhere → 8,200–12,800 over 36.2M = 0.023–0.035% population, 0.018–0.042% GDP → band 7–8. Not fully lawyered.

## Qualitative
- **Strategy 5.** Eger as seat of the Ottoman Eyalet of Eğri (1596–1687) and Hatvan as a sanjak seat (1544–1686); Eger fortress on the frontier; Mátra Power Plant at Visonta with its lignite field (second-largest generator in Hungary); the undeveloped Recsk porphyry copper deposit. No chokepoint or sole command, so no ≥8.
- **Mind 5.** Bishopric of Eger (c. 1004–1009; archbishopric 1804); Eger Lyceum with the 1776 Specula observatory, an attempted university; eponymous Hatvan and Füzesabony Bronze Age cultures; Egri Bikavér. The 1552 siege is event fame and does not count.

## Anchor comparisons
All-time peak (0.11–0.19%): above Potosí c. 1600 (R4, 0.15–0.2% GDP) only at the top of the range, otherwise just below it, and below HU332 Békés (0.22–0.41%). Documented-era peak (0.018–0.021%): above Cahokia (R6, 0.014%), below HU332's 1900 GDP (0.034–0.039%).

## Flags
- Same floor fragility as HU332: on a 7M denominator the peak is 0.08–0.11%, still band 10; on a HYDE-type 14M about 0.05%, band 9.
- Thin lawyering (delta >3×) for windows 1–2; regional analogies, no county-specific published estimate.
- Boundary smearing: 1900/1910 present-territory figures are my subtraction of the Tiszafüred and Pásztó districts and addition of the Bélapátfalva area; check against KSH's restated series.
- Double-count risk: the Tiszafüred district is excluded from every window here and belongs to HU322; the Pásztó area belongs to Nógrád (HU313), which is not on the list; the Bélapátfalva area is counted here and must not be counted under Borsod-Abaúj-Zemplén.
- Ruling D: band 10 only in window 1; band 6–7 held c. 1800–1960.
