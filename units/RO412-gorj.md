# RO412 · Gorj County (Județul Gorj), Romania

**UNIT** RO412 (NUTS-3) = Gorj County.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (Law 2/1968): 5,602 km², seat Târgu Jiu; municipalities Târgu Jiu, Motru; towns Bumbești-Jiu, Novaci, Rovinari, Târgu Cărbunești, Țicleni, Tismana, Turceni; 61 communes. The Getic sub-Carpathians and piedmont of upper Oltenia on the Jiu, Gilort, Motru and Amaradia, with the Parâng and Vâlcan mountains in the north and the Jiu defile. Historic components: the interwar Gorj county (4,579 km²; 206,339 in 1930) plus the Baia de Aramă–Motru area from Mehedinți and the Amaradia villages from Dolj. Pre-1948 figures are allocations to the current territory; the INS current-territory series begins in 1948. Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: Sălcuța–Gumelnița settlement of the Jiu, Gilort, Motru and Amaradia terraces and the piedmont basins.
**Documented-era peak** Window 11, 1850: the Oltenian sub-Carpathian county at its relative demographic height before the world's population surge, with Târgu Jiu as a market town and the Tismana and Polovragi estates; band 7 at the lawyered top (range 6–7) on both pathways; 1870–1912 tie on GDP at the top of range.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 5,602 km² (INS); the Parâng and Vâlcan mountains (~1,300 km²) and the forested high piedmont are excluded: usable terraces, basins and piedmont valleys 3,500–4,200 km².
2. Cultures at the moment: Sălcuța (the Oltenian facies of the Gumelnița complex) on the Jiu and Gilort terraces and in the Târgu Jiu and Tismana basins, with Vinča contacts up the Motru; the piedmont's salt springs and the Jiu defile route to Transylvania.
3. Density 2–3 persons/km² on the usable area, the high end of Müller's (2013) regional Neolithic–Chalcolithic densities by the settlement-area method of Zimmermann, Hilpert & Wendt (2009). Equivalence rule applied. → 7,000–12,600, reported 7–12.6k.
4. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2 (default agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: farming densities on the Parâng and Vâlcan; killed any county total above ~17,000 (>4/km² usable).
- Survives: 12,600 people means ~60–125 villages and hamlets of 100–200 along the Jiu, Gilort, Motru and Amaradia at 4–7 km spacing; cropland need under 3% of the usable area.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across Europe's farmed zone c. 4500 BCE gives a few million Europeans against a 5M world floor, a third to a half of humanity. Floor artefact shared with every farmed Old World unit.

## Deep score — Window 11, 1850 (documented-era peak)

Numerator components
1. Population 150–175k on the current territory: the Wallachian censuses of 1838 and 1859–60 for Gorj county (the Jiul de Sus county of the Organic Statute) with the Mehedinți and Dolj additions, scaled to 1850; Târgu Jiu was a town of 3–4k and the county among Wallachia's most densely settled hill districts (free peasant moșneni villages of the sub-Carpathians).

## Qualitative dimensions
- **Strategy 6.** Nameable but national: the Jiu defile, the pass road from Oltenia to Transylvania (the Roman road, the 1916 battle of the Jiu, the Bumbești–Livezeni railway of 1948); the Oltenia lignite basin and the Rovinari and Turceni plants, a third of Romania's electricity in the 1980s; Tudor Vladimirescu's rising of 1821 began at Padeș. No continental chokepoint, so 6.
- **Mind 8.** Nameable claim of world scale: Constantin Brâncuși, born at Hobița (1876), a founder of modern sculpture, and his Târgu Jiu ensemble (the Table of Silence, the Gate of the Kiss and the Endless Column, 1937–38), a landmark of twentieth-century art; also Nicodim's Tismana (1377–78), Polovragi, the Arjoci wooden churches and the Târgu Jiu school of Ecaterina Teodoroiu's memory. One world-scale artist and his monument carry the peg to 8.

## Flags
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is the Window 1 floor artefact. The documented-era record is a flat plateau: band 6 with the top of range touching band 7 from 1850 to 1912 on both pathways; no window from 1850 to today separates from the others by more than a band.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 only (~5×); the 1850–1912 figures are 1–1.3×.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-1948 figures rebuild the polygon from interwar Gorj plus the Mehedinți and Dolj additions (±15k, 5–6%); the 1930 figure is a reconstruction (the interwar county itself had 206,339).
- **Double-count risk.** RO413 Mehedinți (Baia de Aramă and the Motru valley), RO411 Dolj (the Amaradia and Craiova's hinterland), RO423 Hunedoara (the Jiu valley coal above the defile), RO415 Vâlcea (Novaci–Polovragi border villages).
- **Citation flags.** The 1850–1912 county totals are reconstructions from the Wallachian and Romanian census summaries; no 1930 current-territory figure was retrieved.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 6–7 held 1850–1930 on population and 1850–1912 on GDP; band 6 on GDP again 1966–1992 and on PPP GDP today.
