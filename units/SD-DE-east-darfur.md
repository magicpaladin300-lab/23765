# SD-DE · East Darfur State (Wilayat Sharq Darfur), Sudan

**UNIT** SD-DE (ISO 3166-2) = East Darfur State. Scale flag (C14): an ADM1 unit of ~55,000 km², eight to eleven times a NUTS-3 county; every share below carries the polygon-size caveat.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (state created January 2012 from South Darfur): ~55,000 km², seat Ed Daein; localities Ed Daein, Abu Karinka, Adila, Bahr al-Arab, Assalaya, Shearia, Yassin, Al-Firdous, Abu Jabra. Dar Rizeigat, the Baggara savanna from the Nyala–Babanusa line to the Bahr al-Arab and the South Sudan border (Samaha, the Kiir). Historic components: the Rizeigat dar under the Darfur sultanate, Zubayr Rahma's base at Shakka (1874), the Southern Darfur District of the condominium and of Darfur Province (1956–94), South Darfur State (1994–2012). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the African Humid Period savanna of the Baggara belt at the equivalence-rule densities.
**Documented-era peak** Window 16, 2018–2024: the state's 2018 population of 1,587,200 (the higher published figure; the Central Bureau of Statistics projection gives 1,119,451) carried to 1.8–1.95M in 2024, with Ed Daein at about 300,000; band 7 (administrative/census-grade). Dar Rizeigat under the sultanate c. 1790 never exceeds band 7.
**WINNING PATHWAY** Population (GDP tied in Window 1; GDP band 4 today).

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area ~55,000 km² of Baggara savanna with today's 500–800 mm of rain, wooded grassland in the African Humid Period, with the Bahr al-Arab plain wetter still.
2. Communities at the moment: Sahelian Neolithic herders and cultivators; Dar Rizeigat is archaeologically unexplored, so the equivalence rule for a non-literate society applies at the high end.
3. Density, equivalence rule, high end: 0.15–0.4 persons/km² on the savanna (Hassan's Saharan–Sahelian Neolithic range, C14); no highland or riverine core. → 8,250–22,000, reported 8.25–22k.
4. GDP multiplier 0.8–1.0 (pastoral-agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: European farmed-zone densities anywhere; killed a total above ~28,000 (>0.5/km² over the whole state).
- Survives: 22,000 people means ~110–220 camps and hamlets of 100 along the wadis and the Bahr al-Arab; grazing and cropland need under 5% of the polygon.
- Allocation flag: no excavated site in the polygon anchors the figure.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across the humid-period Sahara and Sahel gives 1–2 million people against a 5M world floor, a fifth to two fifths of humanity.

## Flags
- **Scale (C14).** An ADM1 state of ~55,000 km²; at NUTS-3 scale its shares would be an eighth to an eleventh. Every band above is a state-level band.
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is a Window 1 allocation on an unexplored landscape. The documented-era record rests on the higher of two conflicting 2018 figures (1,587,200 against the CBS 1,119,451) and on the 2024 trend; on the CBS figure alone 2018 is band 6. No pre-modern window exceeds band 7.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 (~6–7×). The contemporary figures are 1.0–1.4×.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-2012 figures are allocations from South Darfur, Darfur Province and the sultanate; the area is the published 55,000 km².
- **Double-count risk.** SD-DS South Darfur (the 2008 parent state), SD-GK West Kordofan (Babanusa and the Misseriya), South Sudan's Northern Bahr el Ghazal (the Bahr al-Arab frontier, Samaha, the Kiir).
- **Citation flags.** The 2008 current-territory figure, the 2024 population and the GDP shares are estimates; the two 2018 figures conflict; no sultanate population figure was retrieved.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 7 on population 2018–2024 (on the higher figure); band 6 from 1973.
