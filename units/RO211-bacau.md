# RO211 · Bacău County (Județul Bacău), Romania

**UNIT** RO211 (NUTS-3) = Bacău County.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (Law 2/1968): 6,621 km², seat Bacău; municipalities Bacău, Moinești, Onești; towns Buhuși, Comănești, Dărmănești, Slănic-Moldova, Târgu Ocna; 85 communes. The Nemira, Tarcău and Ciuc mountains in the west (~35%), the Trotuș and Tazlău Subcarpathians with the Moinești oil, Comănești coal and Târgu Ocna salt, the Siret valley and the western Bârlad plateau. Historic components: the interwar Bacău County (seat Bacău) with the Trotuș valley, plus parts of Putna (Onești area), Roman and Tecuci counties. 1950–1968 the territory was the Bacău Region's core (polygon not used). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: Cucuteni A settlement of the Subcarpathian valleys and the Siret (Poduri-Dealul Ghindaru).
**Documented-era peak** Window 10, c. 1650: the Târgu Ocna salt monopoly, the Trotuș customs road and the Catholic bishopric of Bacău on Willcox's floor; band 8 at the lawyered top (range 6–8). Census-grade peak 1850–1977 (band 7).

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 6,621 km² (INS); the mountain third is excluded: usable Subcarpathian valleys, Siret terraces and plateau 4,000–4,500 km².
2. Cultures at the moment: Cucuteni A in its Subcarpathian heartland: Poduri-Dealul Ghindaru (the deeply stratified Precucuteni–Cucuteni tell with the "Council of the Goddesses"), Târgu Ocna-Podei, Mărgineni-Cetățuia, Fulgeriș and the Siret-terrace sites; the Târgu Ocna salt springs exploited from the Neolithic (Lunca and Cucuieți briquetage lie just across the Neamț line).
3. Density 2–3 persons/km² on the usable area, the high end of Müller's (2013) regional Neolithic–Chalcolithic densities by the settlement-area method of Zimmermann, Hilpert & Wendt (2009). Equivalence rule applied. → 8,000–13,500, reported 8–13k.
4. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2 (default agrarian; salt production attested but unquantified).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: farming densities on the mountain third; killed any county total above ~18,000 (>4/km² usable).
- Survives: 13,000 people means ~65–130 villages of 100–200 along the Trotuș, Tazlău, Siret and Bistrița valleys at 5–8 km spacing, within the recorded Cucuteni site density; cropland need under 3% of the usable area.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across Europe's farmed zone c. 4500 BCE gives a few million Europeans against a 5M world floor, a third to a half of humanity. Floor artefact shared with every farmed Old World unit.

## Deep score — Window 10, c. 1650 (documented-era peak)

Numerator components
1. County population 45–80k: Moldavia at 0.5–0.8M, the Bacău and Trotuș districts among its more populous, with the towns of Bacău (the Catholic see from 1607), Târgu Trotuș (the old customs town on the Transylvanian road through the Ghimeș pass) and Târgu Ocna.
2. GDP multiplier per C12: 1.4–1.8 (between the rubric's regional-centre and entrepôt bands, argued for Moldavia's chief salt mine, a princely monopoly attested from 1380 and exported north to Poland and east across the Prut, together with the Trotuș customs on the Transylvanian trade) × Eastern Europe's per-capita ratio to the world 0.9–1.0 → 1.26–1.8. Cantemir's *Descriptio Moldaviae* (1716) is the named source describing the Ocna salt as the principality's revenue mainstay; its figures were not read in this session, so the 2.0–2.5 band is not applied.

Denominator: 470M at 1650 (Willcox 1931, C2 deep floor, citation flag open).

Result: population 0.0096–0.0170% (band 6–7); GDP 0.0121–0.0306% (band 6–8; 8 only at the extreme top).

Veto notes
- Killed: a Ruling B valuation of the salt at world prices (negligible); killed counting the Moldavian salt exported through the county as more than the county's own extraction and handling margin.

## Flags
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is the Window 1 floor artefact. The documented-era record reaches band 8 only at the extreme top of the c. 1650 range on an argued salt-and-customs premium; the census-grade plateau (1850–1977) is a steady band 7.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 only (~4–5×); the c. 1650 figure is ~2–3×.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-1948 figures are reconstructions across the Bacău, Putna, Roman and Tecuci counties (±15k).
- **Double-count risk.** RO214 Neamț (the Cucuteni and Carpi heartland continues north; the Lunca salt springs), RO226 Vrancea (the Putna remainder and Oituz), RO124 Harghita and RO123 Covasna (the passes' far sides), RO216 Vaslui (Poienești).
- **Citation flags.** Cantemir's salt figures and the Moinești 1930s share were not read; the 2022 GDP is inferred from the county's share.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 7 held c. 1850–1977 on population and c. 1650–1912 on GDP (upper range).
