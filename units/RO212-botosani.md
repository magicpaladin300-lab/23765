# RO212 · Botoșani County (Județul Botoșani), Romania

**UNIT** RO212 (NUTS-3) = Botoșani County.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (Law 2/1968): 4,986 km², seat Botoșani; municipalities Botoșani and Dorohoi; towns Bucecea, Darabani, Flămânzi, Săveni, Ștefănești; 71 communes. The Moldavian Plain of the Jijia, Sitna and Bașeu between the Siret and the Prut, all habitable hill and plain country. Historic components: the interwar counties of Botoșani and Dorohoi (less Dorohoi's Herța district, ceded in 1940 and now in Ukraine) with the Hârlău margin given to Iași. 1950–1968 the territory sat in the Suceava Region (polygon not used). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: Cucuteni A in its northern heartland (Trușești-Țuguieta, Drăgușeni).
**Documented-era peak** Window 11, 1850 (Willcox floor), band 7; the population share holds band 7 from 1850 to 1948.
**WINNING PATHWAY** GDP in Window 1 (population tied); population in the documented era.

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 4,986 km² (INS), all plain and hill country; usable area taken as the whole polygon.
2. Cultures at the moment: Cucuteni A in the Jijia–Prut heartland. Trușești-Țuguieta on the Jijia, one of the largest fully excavated Cucuteni A villages (98 houses, Petrescu-Dîmbovița 1951–61), Drăgușeni-Ostrov (Cucuteni A4), Ripiceni and the dozens of Cucuteni findspots of the Botoșani repertory; the Paleolithic of Mitoc and Ripiceni lies beneath.
3. Density 2.2–3.7 persons/km², the top of the Cucuteni core's published range (as for Iași): a quarter of the recorded Cucuteni findspots occupied at once at Trușești-scale villages of 300–600 gives 15–30k, so 11–18k is adopted. Equivalence rule applied.
4. GDP multiplier 1.0–1.2 (default agrarian).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: the Trypillia giant-settlement densities of the Ukrainian forest-steppe (4100–3600 BCE); any county total above ~25,000 (>5/km²).
- Survives: 18,000 people means ~60–90 villages of 200–300 at 5–7 km spacing across the Jijia, Sitna, Bașeu and Prut valleys, within the recorded site density; cropland need under 4% of the polygon.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking across Europe's farmed zone c. 4500 BCE gives a few million Europeans against a 5M world floor, a third to a half of humanity. Floor artefact shared with every farmed Old World unit.

## Deep score — Window 11, 1850 (documented-era peak)

Numerator components
1. Moldavia's 1859 census counted the Botoșani and Dorohoi districts at roughly 220–240k; the polygon holds about 90% (Herța and the Hârlău margin excluded) and Moldavia grew slowly in the 1850s → 190–215k in 1850. Botoșani town, the "town of the lady princesses" whose revenues went to the prince's consort, held about 25k, the second town of Moldavia, with its great fairs and Jewish, Armenian and Lipovan merchant communities.
2. GDP per head: Moldavia against the world 0.9–1.1 × county index 0.9–1.1 (the Botoșani fairs and grain trade against a poor peasantry) → 0.81–1.21× world.

Denominator: 1,091M (Willcox 1931, the lowest published 1850 total; C2 deep floor, citation flag open).

Result: population 0.0174–0.0197% (band 7); GDP 0.0141–0.0238% (band 6–7).

## Flags
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is the Window 1 floor artefact with the Cucuteni-core density. The documented-era record is a flat band-7 plateau on the population pathway (1850–1948); GDP reaches 7 only at the top of range.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 only (~4×).
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-1948 figures are reconstructions; the Herța district's exclusion is ±10k.
- **Double-count risk.** RO213 Iași (the Cucuteni heartland and the Hârlău margin), RO215 Suceava (the Siret margin), Ukraine's Herța district (not on the list).
- **Citation flags.** The 1948–2021 series is cited from the standard county summaries and not re-read from the INS table this session; the 2022 GDP is derived from the per-capita figure.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 only in Window 1; band 7 population held 1850–1948.
