# SD-GZ · Gezira State (Wilayat al-Jazira), Sudan

**UNIT** SD-GZ (ISO 3166-2) = Gezira State. Scale flag (C14): an ADM1 unit of 27,549 km², four to six times a NUTS-3 county; every share below carries the polygon-size caveat.
**Polygon** Current boundaries (state of 1994): 27,549 km², seat Wad Madani; localities Wad Madani, Greater Madani, South Gezira, East Gezira (Rufaa), Al-Kamlin, Al-Hasaheisa, Al-Managil, Umm al-Qura. The Gezira between the Blue and White Niles from the Khartoum line to the Sennar line, the irrigated Gezira and Managil schemes, and the Blue Nile towns of Rufaa, Arbaji and Al-Hasaheisa. Historic components: the heartland of Alodia (whose capital Soba lies just over the Khartoum line) and of the Funj sultanate (Arbaji, founded 1475), the Turco-Egyptian and condominium Blue Nile Province (Wad Madani), Blue Nile Province of 1956–74 and Central Region (1974–94). Boundary divergences of one year or less: none material.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment** Window 1 (Chalcolithic), c. 4500 BCE: the Khartoum Neolithic fisher-herders of both Nile banks and the humid-period Gezira plain at the equivalence-rule densities.
**Documented-era peak** Window 10, c. 1650: the Funj sultanate's Gezira heartland under Badi II Abu Daqn, with Arbaji (the sultanate's second town, founded 1475) and the Blue Nile market towns, a third to two fifths of the Funj core; band 10 at the lawyered top (range 8–10, Willcox's 470M floor). Census-grade peak: band 9 (population 2008–2024: 3,575,280 at the 2008 census, 5,096,920 in the 2018 projection), the gallery's highest true-census share; GDP band 8–9 in the Gezira cotton era (1956–1993).

## Deep score — Window 1, c. 4500 BCE

Numerator components
1. Area 27,549 km²: the Blue Nile and White Nile banks and terraces (~3,000 km²) and the Gezira clay plain (~24,500 km²), savanna in the African Humid Period.
2. Communities at the moment: the Khartoum Neolithic (Shaheinab horizon) on both Niles, with the Rabak and Shabona sites just south and the Khartoum sites just north of the polygon, herder-fisher-cultivators; the equivalence rule for a non-literate society applies at the high end.
3. Density, equivalence rule, high end: 1–2 persons/km² on the river strips, 0.15–0.4 on the plain (Hassan's Saharan–Sahelian Neolithic range, C14). → 3–6k on the rivers, 3.7–9.8k on the plain, 6.7–15.8k in all.
4. GDP multiplier 0.8–1.1 (riverine herder-cultivators with cattle wealth).

Denominator: 5.0M at 4500 BCE (rubric floors table; McEvedy & Jones 5M at 5000 BCE, 7M at 4000 BCE).

Veto notes
- Killed: Nile-floodplain densities above 2/km² for a fisher-herder horizon; killed a total above ~20,000.
- Survives: 15,800 people means ~80–160 villages, camps and hamlets of 100, two fifths of them on the rivers at 3–5 km spacing.
- Allocation flag: the plain is an allocation; the river figure rests on the Khartoum Neolithic site lists.

Nesting flag (C11): the same stacking along the Nile and across the humid-period Sahel gives 2–3 million people against a 5M world floor, roughly half of humanity.

## Deep score — Window 10, c. 1650 (documented-era peak)

Numerator components
1. Built numerators: Arbaji (Hellet Amara), founded in 1475 and the sultanate's second town, a trading and scholarly centre that Krump (1701) and Bruce (1772) describe as large, 5–10k; Rufaa, Al-Hasaheisa, Al-Kamlin and the Blue Nile market villages 10–20k. Towns 15–30k.
2. Countryside: the rain-fed sorghum and cattle villages of the Gezira between the Niles, the sultanate's granary and the densest part of its core, a third to two fifths of a Funj core lawyered at 0.8–1.2M → 185–420k. County 200–450k.
3. GDP multiplier 0.8–1.2: Maddison's Africa ratio (~0.7 of world c. 1650) × role premium 1.1–1.7 relative to the region for the granary and second town of the largest state of the Nilotic Sudan, with the Blue Nile trade between Sennar and the Khartoum–Shendi reach (no capital premium, which belongs to Sennar; no ledger, so the 2.0–2.5 band is not claimed).

Denominator: 470M at 1650 (Willcox 1931; C2 deep floor); McEvedy & Jones's 545M would lower the shares by 14%.

## Flags
- **Scale (C14).** An ADM1 state of 27,549 km²; at NUTS-3 scale its shares would be a quarter to a sixth. Every band above is a state-level band.
- **Ambiguity.** The all-time peak is a Window 1 allocation anchored on the Khartoum Neolithic. The documented-era record (c. 1650) is band 10 only at the top of range (450k people and the 1.2× multiplier on Willcox's floor), band 9 at the mid-range; Alodia c. 900 would match it but has no built numerator inside the polygon. The census-grade record is band 9 from 2008 on a true census and holds on the CBS projection; the 2024 figure precedes the war's displacement.
- **Thin lawyering (delta >3×).** Window 1 (~6–7×); the c. 1650 GDP figure (~3.5–4×). The census-grade figures are 1.0–1.2×.
- **Boundary smearing.** Pre-1956 figures are allocations from Blue Nile Province and the Funj core; the Funj hinterland is a judgement share.
- **Double-count risk.** SD-SI Sennar (the Funj capital and the scheme's dam), SD-KH Khartoum (Soba, the Khartoum reach), SD-NW White Nile (the western Gezira), SD-GD Gedaref (the Rahad and the Butana).
- **Citation flags.** No Funj or Alodian population total was retrieved; the 2024 population and the GDP shares are estimates; the 2018 figure is the Wikipedia-cited CBS projection.
- **Duration metadata (Ruling D).** Band 10 in Window 1 and, at the top of range only, c. 1640–1680; band 8–9 held c. 1550–1760 on the lawyered Funj figures and again 1956–2024 on the census-grade series.
