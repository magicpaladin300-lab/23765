# Mali units — short cards (band and shares only)

Floors: 5.0M (4500 BCE); 230.2M (c. 800); 284.1M (c. 1068); 357.6M (c. 1335); 350M (1450); 523.7M (c. 1584); 7.83B (2022, interpolated).

- North (Taoudénit, Timbuktu, Kidal, Gao, Ménaka): Green Sahara at 0.02/km² × 1.2. Taoudénit and Kidal have no later moment that competes; Taghaza and Taoudenni salt and Tadmekka's gold-coin molds (c. 1000) score on Strategy and Mind only.
- Timbuktu: the "100,000 by 1450, a quarter of them students" reading survives the built-area check only with straw-hut outskirts; ×2.0 as the salt–gold entrepôt. Population headline comes from the 4500 BCE floor (0.082%), which beats the 1450 count (0.071%).
- Gao: 7,626 permanent houses under Askia al-Hajj II (≈ 75,000), outskirts to 100,000, plus the Bourem–Ansongo valley; Songhai imperial capital ×2.0.
- Mopti: Jenne-jeno cluster 10,000–26,000 by 800 CE (still growing to 1200), Dia's city mounds, and the Inland Delta at ≈ 4.5/km².
- Nara and Nioro: Wagadu core of Ghana per al-Bakri (1068); Koumbi Saleh itself lies in Mauritania, so ×1.6 not ×2.0.
- Mali Empire regions (c. 1335): Koulikoro carries the Manden heartland (Kangaba, Kirina, Siby); Kayes carries Bambuk (Kéniéba). Ségou's Bambara-capital moment (Park's 30,000 in 1796) gives 0.092%, below c. 1335.
- Bamako: population pathway only; 2023 GDP share ≈ 35% of Mali's ≈ $21B.
