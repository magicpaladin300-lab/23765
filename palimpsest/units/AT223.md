# AT223 · Östliche Obersteiermark — PALIMPSEST RUBRIC v1.0 card

**UNIT.** AT223 Östliche Obersteiermark, Steiermark, Austria.

**Polygon.** Political districts Leoben (611) and Bruck-Mürzzuschlag (621; merged 2013 from Bruck an der Mur 602 and Mürzzuschlag 613). About 3,250 km². Contains the Erzberg with Eisenerz and Vordernberg, Leoben with the Donawitz works, Kapfenberg with the Böhler works, Bruck an der Mur, Kindberg, Mürzzuschlag at the Styrian foot of the Semmering, and Mariazell. Hieflau and its ÖAMG blast furnace lie in Liezen district (AT222), outside the polygon.

**PEAK WINDOW + moment.** Window 12, High imperial. Moment: 1913. Earliest attainment of the Band 8 range: c. 1870. Duration in Band 8 or above: roughly 1865–1944, with a dip in the 1920s and early 1930s.

**WINNING PATHWAY.** GDP (Maddison Austria series allocated to the polygon with a heavy-industry premium).

**LAWYERED SHARE.** GDP: 0.041–0.052% of world GDP (1913); headline 0.05%. Population: 0.009–0.010% of humanity, reached twice (c. 1530 and 1913).

**CONSENSUS SHARE.** GDP ≈ 0.024% (premium 1.2×, Maddison 2003 world total). Population ≈ 0.0085% (1913, UN-type world total ≈ 1.79B).

**DELTA.** GDP 2.2×; population 1.1×.

**BAND.** Share band 9 — boundary case (headline sits on the 0.05% line; falls to 8 at a 2.0× premium or if Leoben district counted fewer than ~66,000 in 1910). Strategy 6 (1938–45). Mind 7 (1953). Overall 9.

5. 1945 (DP influx, occupation, ore output 318,900 t) — ≤1-year divergence, skipped.
6. 1917 POW labour at the Erzberg and Böhler war employment — unquantified from reachable sources; excluded from headline.
7. Forced-labour inflation beyond ≈10,000 for 1943 — no source; capped.
